# Contributing

We welcome corrections, independent reproductions, relevant literature, and focused mathematical contributions. Open an issue with the exact claim or command, your environment, and reproducible evidence. Reports that a result is already known are valuable contributions.

For a new result, supply the model and source, all parameter assumptions and quantifiers, the proposed contribution relative to prior work, the proof or numerical procedure, and a command that checks the evidence. Label floating-point observations, conditional theorems, and exact conclusions separately.

Run `python scripts/reproduce.py verify` before proposing changes to the phosphorylation project. If the mathematical evidence changes, regenerate the certificates and document the change; do not update only a screenshot or a checksum. Keep each project self-contained under `projects/`.

Expert comments are not automatically endorsements. Quote or identify a reviewer only with their permission. Do not upload private correspondence, credentials, patient information, or third-party papers.
