Gross, Krone and Martin, Dimensions of Level-1 Group-Based Phylogenetic Networks, Bulletin of Mathematical Biology86:90(2024), [publisher](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11538-024-01314-z). Section2.2 defines group-consistent leaf labels and orbit-shared edge parameters: there are |G|^(n−1) consistent labels at n leaves. Proposition15 requires a B-orbit-constant weight for tropical transfer. Its all-B sunlet formula covers at least four leaves, so does not establish the cyclic-inversion 3-sunlet theorem. Corollary10, Lemma11 and Theorem17 provide the existing gluing/tree/larger-sunlet ingredients for the stated B={id} level-1 consequence; Cox2025 Theorem3.8 supplies that induction. The 2024 Conjecture25 local m rendering remains ambiguous; no arbitrary-B resolution is asserted here.

The general-model even-order arguments and cyclic-inversion argument supply complete uniform characteristic-zero kernel proofs. Source weights, bounds, known general-model finite cases and the source level-1 induction are credited. The inversion model is defined by the actual raw q² labels, not by assuming an orbit-pair quotient preserves the map or rank. The new contribution is its uniform strict orbit-distance chamber and kernel theorem.
