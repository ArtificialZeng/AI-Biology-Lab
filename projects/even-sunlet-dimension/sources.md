# Primary sources and bounded comparison

Cox, Gross and Martin, *Group-based phylogenetic models on 3-sunlet networks*, Bulletin of Mathematical Biology87:132 (2025), [publisher](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11538-025-01506-1), [arXiv:2409.17894](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.17894). Theorem1.1 proves the odd-order q≥5 result. Section2.1 gives the map; Lemma2.1 the upper bound; Section2.2 the tropical lower bound; Lemma2.3 the strict selection inequalities. Section4.4/Table7 supplies finite even examples, including elementary-two r=3,4,5. Section5 discusses the remaining even-order problem and separately leaves nontrivial B unexplored.

The exponent-greater-than-two proof follows the source's modified odd-order weight pattern, including nonzero involutions in X. That weight pattern and the source's finite cases are credited. Its contribution is the complete uniform kernel argument. The separate elementary-two proof supplies a Hamming-order chamber and a complete all-r kernel argument. Together these cover every even-order group of order at least6, and the credited odd-order theorem gives the stated all-group result for q≥5.

Gross, Krone and Martin, *Dimensions of Level-1 Group-Based Phylogenetic Networks*, Bulletin of Mathematical Biology86:90 (2024), [publisher](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11538-024-01314-z). Its published Corollary10, Lemma11 and Theorem17 provide the existing cut-edge formula, binary-tree dimension and k≥4 sunlet dimensions. Cox–Gross–Martin Theorem3.8 supplies the existing induction for the credited level-1 consequence. None of these gluing steps is claimed as a new method.
