## Abstract

Recent benchmarks report that simple linear regression matches or outperforms deep learning models in predicting single-gene CRISPR perturbation transcriptomes. Here we show that this paradox reflects a **metric inversion** between two distinct statistical tasks: predicting inter-perturbation amplitude along a dominant shared transcriptional variation axis versus predicting perturbation-specific regulatory responses. Even **after** subtracting the mean perturbation response (Systema perturbation-centroid centering, algebraically identical to our $\Delta$-expression Pearson metric `delta_r` on all three atlases), linear models (`AE` bilinear and `S0` SVD-ridge) continue to outperform cross-line residual methods because linear regression exploits perturbation-magnitude variation along the dominant shared stress axis (PC1 of training residuals). Conversely, when evaluating perturbation-specific signals—either by projecting out that dominant shared axis (`v5` orthogonal-residual Pearson) or by testing unprojected perturbation identification ("which specific gene was perturbed?", raw-space perturbation discrimination `disc_raw`)—method rankings invert in favor of our frozen, zero-pretraining cross-line kernel model (**`S5`, Cross-Line Perturb-Bridge**, built on soft-power HybridTOM $p=1.5$ kernels and GeneSlope-CosKRR cross-line translation).

After freezing all solver constants on BioXArena-Pro `xatlas-orion-perturb` (where a single frozen Codabench private test submission scored **`0.0998`** [`Codabench官方结果_submission1062221.html`], highest on the task and `+0.0173` above the best 2-hour autonomous agent `0.0825`, within the `0.02` noise threshold), we evaluated zero-shot across two independent laboratories' Perturb-seq atlases under strict simultaneous union holdout (10 splits, 4,000-resample cell-line-stratified row-level paired bootstrap 95% CIs). Relative to linear baselines (`AE`, `S0`), simplified neural baselines, and official **`cell-gears==0.1.2`** (10 splits each on Replogle 2022 and Nadig 2025; `S5 − Official_GEARS`: `v5 = +0.1096` / `+0.1164`, `disc_raw = +0.1263` / `+0.1057`, **`10/10` splits CI `> 0`** on both datasets [`reports/r72_official_gears_{replogle,nadig}_summary.json`]), frozen `S5` achieved consistent gains on perturbation-specific metrics across all three datasets. In a **pre-registered, zero-look confirmatory test** locked in git prior to expression inspection (`reports/ROUND7_PREREGISTRATION.md`, commits `c5294c0`, `87f4bc2`, `b9fe2bb`), all three hypotheses were **confirmed** on unseen Nadig et al. (*Nature Genetics* 2025, `GSE264667` HepG2 + Jurkat): primary **H1** (`disc_raw` `S5 − AE = +0.0487`, `9/10` splits CI `> 0`, with modest lower bounds near zero), primary **H2** (`delta_r` `S5 − AE = −0.0195`, `9/10` splits negative and `4/10` CI `< 0`, weaker than Replogle's `8/10`), and secondary **H3** (`v5` `S5 − AE = +0.0595`, `10/10` splits CI `> 0` [`reports/r71_nadig_confirmation_summary.json`]). However, benchmarked against a **linear ridge probe on frozen 33M-cell pretrained `scGPT_human` gene embeddings (`scGPT_FrozenEmb_Ridge`)**—our strongest baseline across all datasets—zero-pretraining `S5` outperforms the foundation-model probe **only on `v5` orthogonal residuals** (`+0.0274`, `10/10` CI `> 0` on Replogle; `+0.0230`, `7/10` CI `> 0` on Nadig), **statistically ties on `disc_raw`** (`+0.0025` / `+0.0043`, `0/10` CI significant either way), and **trails substantially on `delta_r`** (`−0.0609` / `−0.0627`, `10/10` CI `< 0` [`reports/r72_scgpt_probe_{replogle,nadig}_row_ci.csv`]). Pathway analysis strictly on the evaluated gene universe shows `S7 − S0` and `S5 − S2` gains enrich **mRNA splicing** (`FDR = 1.18e-08`, `3.12×`) and **mitochondrial translation** (`FDR = 2.76e-03`, `2.90×`; `0/50` random controls [`reports/t5_local_reactome_enrichment_summary.json`]). We openly report all unfavorable results, including a non-PSD kernel collapse vulnerability when `trans_pk < 1`, potential CELLxGENE pretraining corpus overlap for `scGPT_human`, compute blockers for full `scGPT`/`STATE` fine-tuning, and unexcluded CRISPRi *cis*-repression confounding for Stage S6 on anonymized xatlas.

## 1. Introduction: The Metric Debate and Problem Formulation

Predicting transcriptome-wide responses to unseen single-gene CRISPR perturbations is a central goal of virtual-cell modeling. Yet recent systematic benchmarks (Ahlmann-Eltze et al., *Nature Methods* 2025; Viñas Torné et al. / Systema, *Nature Biotechnology* 2025; Adduri et al. / STATE, 2025) revealed that complex deep learning models frequently fail to outperform simple linear regression or perturbation-mean baselines under standard Pearson correlation metrics.

The systemic-variation literature (Systema) demonstrated that genetic perturbations share a large non-specific shift (general stress, proliferation slowdown, and metabolic attenuation) relative to unperturbed controls, inflating uncentered correlation scores. Subtracting the **perturbation centroid** (mean response across perturbations) removes this constant shared shift. Here we show across three independent Perturb-seq atlases that **centroid subtraction alone does not isolate perturbation-specific biology**: even after removing the perturbation mean response, perturbations vary markedly in their scalar *amplitude* along the **dominant shared inter-perturbation variation axis (PC1 of training residuals)**. Linear regression (`AE` bilinear and `S0` SVD-ridge) fits raw post-perturbation vectors directly and therefore excels at reconstructing scalar intensity along this dominant shared axis—achieving higher centroid-centered Pearson (`delta_r` $\equiv$ `centroid_train_r`) and Top-50 DE correlation (`de50_r`), while producing more generic profiles that blur individual gene identities.

We formalize single-gene perturbation prediction across paired human cell lines $(A, B)$ around two distinct evaluation objectives:
1. **Centroid-centered & Top-DE reconstruction** (`delta_r` $\equiv$ `centroid_train_r`, `centroid_val_r`, `de50_r`, `de50_sign`): Accuracy of gene-expression reconstruction relative to the mean perturbation centroid.
2. **Perturbation-specific signal recovery**:
   - **Orthogonal-residual Pearson (`v5`)**: Pearson correlation on held-out genes after QR-projecting both true and predicted profiles orthogonal to $\mathrm{span}\{\mathbf{1}, \mathbf{b}_c, \mathbf{a}_c\}$, where $\mathbf{b}_c$ is the reproducible training perturbation centroid and $\mathbf{a}_c$ is the dominant shared stress axis (PC1 of training residuals), excluding the perturbed target gene itself.
   - **Raw-space perturbation discrimination (`disc_raw`, plus `disc_v5`)**: Independent of `v5`, in **unprojected** space, how high does the true target $y_i$ rank among all $M$ held-out perturbations $\{y_j\}_{j=1}^M$ when retrieved by Pearson correlation with $\hat{y}_i$? Formally, $\mathrm{disc\_raw}(i) = 1 - \frac{1}{M-1}\sum_{j=1}^M \mathbb{I}[\mathrm{corr}(\hat{y}_i - \mathbf{b}_c, y_j - \mathbf{b}_c) > \mathrm{corr}(\hat{y}_i - \mathbf{b}_c, y_i - \mathbf{b}_c)] \in [0, 1]$ (`0.50` = random, `1.00` = perfect #1 retrieval).

Our findings establish a clean boundary: relative to linear baselines (`AE`, `S0`) and both simplified and official graph-neural baselines (`Simplified_GNN`, official `cell-gears==0.1.2`), our frozen cross-line kernel model (`S5`) achieves consistent, pre-registered gains on perturbation-specific metrics (`v5`, `disc_raw`); relative to a 33M-cell pretrained foundation-model embedding probe (`scGPT_FrozenEmb_Ridge`), `S5` retains a significant advantage on `v5` orthogonal residuals, ties on `disc_raw`, and trails substantially on `delta_r`—pointing directly to a testable complementarity hypothesis for future pre-registered work.

## 2. Methods

### 2.1 Core Architecture: Soft-Power HybridTOM Kernel and Cross-Line Perturb-Bridge (`S5`, Primary Method)
All solver constants are permanently frozen at `src/xatlas_panhuman_solver.py` (commit `3609811`, bit-for-bit identical to the Codabench submission pack [`outputs/perturbation_baselines/xatlas-orion-perturb/CODABENCH_FREEZE_MANIFEST.json`]). Given paired perturbation matrices across two cell lines $(A, B)$ sharing readout genes $\mathcal{G}_{\mathrm{sh}}$, primary method **`S5`** executes without gradient descent:

1. **Leak-Free Training Subspace (`fit_baseline_and_axis`)**: On each split's training set for cell line $c \in \{A, B\}$, set baseline $\mathbf{b}_c$ to the mean of reproducible training perturbations and shared axis $\mathbf{a}_c$ to the unit-norm first right singular vector of centered reproducible training residuals. Project training targets orthogonal to $\mathrm{span}\{\mathbf{1}, \mathbf{b}_c, \mathbf{a}_c\}$ (`project_rows_5000`).
2. **Self-Masked, Precision-Weighted Soft-Power HybridTOM Kernel (`S1` Core)**: Form baseline-subtracted, $\sqrt{n_{\mathrm{cells}}}$-weighted training matrices with each perturbed target gene's self-knockdown entry masked to zero (`self_mask`). Compute gene-gene cosine similarity $G^{(0)}$ across training perturbations, then construct the signed soft-power HybridTOM kernel ($p = 1.5$):
   $$K_{\mathrm{HybridTOM}}(G; p) = \frac{1}{2}\Big(\mathrm{sign}(G)|G|^p + \mathrm{TOM}\big(\mathrm{sign}(G)|G|^p\big)\Big),$$
   combining direct gene-gene co-variation with higher-order topological neighborhood overlap (`compute_hybrid_tom_from_G`).
3. **Multi-Scale Spectral Deflation (`S2`) & Pan-Human Cross-Line Gene Graph (`S3`)**: Deflate the top $k \in \{0, 1, 2, 5, 10\}$ principal components of each line's training matrix (`PC_WEIGHTS = {0:1.0, 1:0.8, 2:0.5, 5:0.3, 10:0.2}`) and fuse the partner line's shared-gene correlation pyramid (`PAN_OTHER_WEIGHTS = {0:0.6, 1:0.8, 2:0.8, 5:0.6, 10:0.5}`).
4. **Empirical-SNR Non-Reproducible Anchors (`S4`)**: Include weak/non-reproducible measured training perturbations (`reproducible == 0`) with down-weighted SNR weights $w_{\mathrm{nr}} = 0.25 \cdot \mathrm{clip}\big((z_{\mathrm{eff}} - 0.9\,\mathrm{med})/(0.3\,\mathrm{med}), 0.1, 1.5\big)$.
5. **GeneSlope-CosKRR Cross-Line Translator & Symmetric Bridge (`S5`)**: Fit a bidirectional nonlinear cross-line translator (`fit_geneslope_coskrr_translator`, `skip_w=0.8, alpha=2.0, p_k=1.5`) on shared training perturbations measured reproducibly in both lines, combining gene-wise shrunk slope skips on shared genes with signed-power cosine KRR on 5,000-gene residual profiles. Use it to (i) project partner-only reproducible training perturbations into the target cell line (`extra_weight = 0.6`), and (ii) translate partner-line KRR predictions (`alpha = 0.5`) of held-out queries and fuse them symmetrically (`bridge_w = 0.9`).

### 2.2 Extensions Evaluated as Secondary (`S7`) and Exploratory (`S7_no_S6`)
- **Stage `S6` (Reciprocal Response Borrowing)**: Borrows residual responses from training perturbations that strongly perturb the query gene ($|z| > 4$, quadratic ReLU weight). `S6` attains the single highest `disc_raw` on all three atlases, but is strongly dataset-specific on `v5` (`+0.0130` xatlas vs `−0.0153` Replogle [`reports/t4_lomo_summary.json`]) and cannot rule out CRISPRi *cis*-proximity confounding on anonymized xatlas (`reports/t6_replogle_crispri_proximity_summary.json`).
- **Stage `S7` (Output SVD Wiener Denoising + Softmax Manifold Snapping, Secondary Frozen Method)**: Applies spectral Wiener shrinkage (`tau = 0.020`) and cosine-similarity manifold snapping (`snap_w = 0.45, temp = 18.0, top_k = 40`). `S7` is the exact frozen Codabench submission pipeline; it consistently improves `v5` correlation while reducing `disc_raw`.
- **`S7_no_S6_exploratory` (Strictly Exploratory)**: Omits `S6` from `S7` (`LOO_S6_Reciprocal`). Because its Replogle `v5` gain (`0.1512`) was discovered post-hoc in Round 6, it was pre-registered strictly as exploratory (`reports/ROUND7_PREREGISTRATION.md`).

### 2.3 Datasets, Pre-Registered Preprocessing, Holdout Splits, and Statistical Inference
1. **Development Atlas (`xatlas-orion-perturb`, BioXArena-Pro)**: HCT116 + HEK293T (`5,000` readout genes/line, `4,040` shared; anonymized `g_<hex>` IDs, never de-anonymized). Evaluated on 10 dev splits (`SEEDS_10 = [42, 0, 1, 2, 3, 7, 11, 99, 2024, 31337]`), 10 unseen resplits (`NEW_SEEDS_10 = 101..110`), and one frozen Codabench private test submission (`Submission 1062221`, `455` targets).
2. **External Zero-Shot Atlas 1 (Replogle et al., *Cell* 2022, Figshare+ `10.25452/figshare.plus.20029387`)**: RPE1 (`rpe1_normalized_bulk_01.h5ad`, SHA-256 `a3c5bfd0...`) + K562 (`K562_gwps_normalized_bulk_01.h5ad`, SHA-256 `37e48c47...`); top `5,000` finite genes by `var/clean_mean`; `reproducible = 1` iff `anderson_darling_counts >= 50` AND `energy_test_p_value < 0.001`; deduplicated to 1 row per Ensembl target (`src/prepare_replogle2022_cache.py`).
3. **Pre-Registered Unseen Confirmatory Atlas 2 (Nadig et al., *Nature Genetics* 2025, NCBI GEO `GSE264667`, PMID `40259084`)**: HepG2 (`GSE264667_hepg2_raw_singlecell_01.h5ad`, SHA-256 `e1ad7c3c...`, `145,473 × 9,624`) + Jurkat (`GSE264667_jurkat_raw_singlecell_01.h5ad`, SHA-256 `ffbe15f2...`, `262,956 × 8,882`). Locked prior to reading any expression entry (`Addendum B`, commit `b9fe2bb`): exact Replogle 2022 per-`gem_group` library-size normalization and z-scoring against `non-targeting` control cells, top `5,000` finite genes by `control_mean` (`3,644` shared Ensembl genes), vectorized Scholz-Stephens two-sample Anderson-Darling test (`ad_counts >= 50` at BH FDR `< 0.05`, unit-verified `< 1e-5` vs `scipy.stats.anderson_ksamp`) AND 20-PC 2,000-permutation energy-distance test (`p < 0.001`) with `n_cells >= 25`, yielding `2,343` HepG2 (`1,000` reproducible) and `2,350` Jurkat (`1,262` reproducible) deduplicated Ensembl targets (`src/prepare_nadig2025_cache.py`).

**Strict Simultaneous Union Holdout (`make_strict_union_split`)**: For each seed in `SEEDS_10`, sample 20% of unique reproducible in-readout targets independently per cell line, take their **union**, and exclude all union targets simultaneously from **both** cell lines' training sets (zero cross-line leakage). All metrics are macro-averaged equally (`0.5 × line0 + 0.5 × line1`).

**Row-Level Stratified Paired Bootstrap 95% CIs**: Split-level paired $t$-tests are invalid under overlapping training sets and are permanently excluded. Every paired method comparison reports **4,000-resample cell-line-stratified row-level paired bootstrap 95% CIs** per split (`stratified_row_bootstrap_ci`, `RandomState(12345)`, `src/compute_row_bootstrap_ci.py`) plus the count of splits (out of 10) whose 95% CI excludes zero.

### 3.2 Pre-Registered Unseen External Confirmation on Nadig et al. (2025) (Fig. 2)

### 3.4 Leave-One-Module-Out (LOMO) Transferability & S5 ±50% Hyperparameter Sensitivity (Fig. 3)
Dual-dataset LOMO ablation (`reports/t4_lomo_summary.json`, **Figure 3A**, `docs/figures/fig3_lomo_ablation_and_s5_sensitivity.png`):
- **Transferable core modules (positive on both xatlas and Replogle)**: `S1_HybridTOM` ($p=1.5$: `+0.0109` xatlas [`7/10` CI `> 0`] / `+0.0103` Replogle [`10/10` CI `> 0`]), `S7_WienerSnap` (`+0.0072` / `+0.0067` on `v5`, hurting `disc_raw`), `S5_CrossLineBridge` (`+0.0020` / `+0.0061`, Replogle `9/10` CI `> 0`), and `S2_Deflation` (`+0.0108` / `+0.0035`, Replogle `1/10` CI `> 0`).
- **Dataset-specific modules**: `S6_Reciprocal` (`+0.0130` [`8/10` CI `> 0`] on xatlas vs **`−0.0153` [`9/10` CI `< 0`]** on Replogle; omitting `S6` yields Replogle `v5 = 0.1512`) and `S4_NonRepAnchors` (`+0.0043` vs `−0.0006`). Yet `S6` achieves the highest `disc_raw` on all three datasets (`0.6453`, `0.6993`, `0.7184`), reflecting a consistent specificity-versus-amplitude-noise trade-off.

One-factor-at-a-time **±50% sensitivity** of all 10 frozen constants in primary method `S5` (`reports/r73_sensitivity_{replogle,nadig}_summary.json`, **Figure 3B**):
- **Replogle 2022**: `18/20` perturbed configurations retain `v5` `10/10` CI `> 0` and `disc_raw` `8–10/10` CI `> 0` vs `AE` (`|Δv5| ≤ 0.006`).
- **Nadig 2025**: `20/20` perturbed configurations retain `v5` `10/10` CI `> 0` vs `AE`, and `17/20` retain `disc_raw` `≥ 7/10` CI `> 0` (`7–9/10`).
- **Identified Concave-Power Regime Boundary (`p < 1`)**: Setting `soft_p` to `−50%` (`0.75`) drops `disc_raw` CI `> 0` splits to `5/10` (Replogle) and `6/10` (Nadig). Setting `trans_pk` to `−50%` (`0.75`) causes severe collapse on Replogle (`v5 = 0.0602`, `−0.0798` shift; `disc_raw = 0.6052`, `0/10` CI `> 0`) and the largest drop on Nadig (`v5 = 0.1571`, `−0.0170`). Spectral diagnostics (`Replogle seed 42`) pinpoint the exact mechanism: concave element-wise power `sign(G)|G|^{0.75}` destroys positive semidefiniteness (`min_eig(K) = −6.177`), creating **6 negative eigenvalues** in `K + 2I` (`min_eig = −4.177`, `min_abs_eig = 0.0267`) and inflating the linear-system condition number from `17.2` (`p_k = 1.5`) to **`4,473.5`** (`p_k = 0.75`). Any convex power `p_k ≥ 1.0` preserves strict positive definiteness (`0` negative eigenvalues; `cond = 37.5` at `1.0`, `17.2` at `1.5`, `7.2` at `2.25`). Per protocol, frozen constants were left unchanged.

## 4. Discussion & Testable Future Hypothesis

Our results resolve a central apparent contradiction in perturbation benchmarking while establishing a clear ceiling on what zero-pretraining cross-line kernels achieve alone. Relative to linear baselines (`AE`, `S0`) and both simplified and official graph-neural networks (`Simplified_GNN`, `cell-gears==0.1.2`), frozen `S5` delivers reproducible gains on perturbation-specific signals (`v5` and `disc_raw`) across all three atlases, confirmed pre-registered on unseen Nadig et al. (2025). Yet benchmarking against frozen 33M-cell `scGPT_human` gene embeddings (`scGPT_FrozenEmb_Ridge`) reveals a sharper structural division of labor: **pretrained foundation-model embeddings excel at dominant shared and centroid-relative transcriptional responses (`delta_r`, `de50_r`, `de50_sign`), whereas cross-line nonlinear kernels (`S5`) excel at orthogonal perturbation-specific residuals (`v5`), with both tying on raw perturbation discrimination (`disc_raw`)**.

**Pre-Registerable Complementarity Hypothesis (Deferred to Future Work)**: Because `scGPT_FrozenEmb_Ridge` and `S5` dominate orthogonal subspaces of the perturbation transcriptome—the dominant shared/centroid axis versus the orthogonal perturbation-specific residual subspace—combining pretrained foundation-model gene representations for shared-axis prediction with cross-line HybridTOM/bridge modeling for orthogonal residuals could outperform both approaches simultaneously across all metrics. Strictly adhering to our pre-registration discipline, we **do not** engineer or evaluate any post-hoc hybrid on Replogle 2022 or Nadig 2025 after observing these results; testing this complementarity hypothesis is explicitly deferred to a future pre-registered evaluation on a fourth independent perturbation atlas.

## 5. Limitations

1. **Performance Relative to Pretrained Foundation-Model Embeddings and Traditional Metrics**: Frozen `S5` trails `AE`, `S0`, and `scGPT_FrozenEmb_Ridge` on `delta_r` (`= centroid_train_r`), `centroid_val_r`, `de50_r`, and `de50_sign`, and relative to `scGPT_FrozenEmb_Ridge` wins only on `v5` while tying on `disc_raw` (`0/10` splits significant).
2. **Potential `scGPT_human` Pretraining Corpus Overlap**: `scGPT_human` was pretrained on 33M normal human cells from CELLxGENE (2023). While Nadig et al. (2025, GEO public May 2024) strictly post-dates `scGPT_human` so Nadig perturbation profiles cannot appear in pretraining, Replogle et al. (2022) pre-dates 2023 and broad CELLxGENE gene co-expression overlap across human cell lines cannot be ruled out.
3. **Human-AI Multi-Round Development vs. Autonomous Agents**: The Codabench private test score (`0.0998`) lies within the `0.02` noise threshold of the best 2-hour autonomous agent (`0.0825`) and reflects multi-round iterative design rather than single-shot agent execution.
4. **Modest Raw Discrimination Margins & Split-Level Variability**: On unseen Nadig 2025, several H1 (`disc_raw`) split 95% CI lower bounds lie near zero (`+0.0011`–`+0.0038`, plus `−0.0080` on seed 2024), H2 (`delta_r`) is individually significant on only `4/10` splits (weaker than `8/10` on Replogle), and `S5 − S0` on `disc_raw` is `6/10` CI `> 0`. On Replogle `v5`, `S7` beats `Simplified_GNN` on only `3/10` splits (`S5`: `7/10`), though `Simplified_GNN` underperforms linear baselines on `disc_raw`.
5. **Unexcluded CRISPRi Cis-Proximity Confounding for Stage S6 on Anonymized xatlas**: Because xatlas gene identities remain anonymized and uninspected, local CRISPRi *cis*-repression cannot be ruled out for `S6`'s xatlas-specific gain (`+0.0130`).
6. **Concave Kernel Power Vulnerability (`trans_pk < 1`)**: Setting `trans_pk = 0.75` destroys kernel positive semidefiniteness (`min_eig(K + 2I) = −4.177`, `cond = 4,473.5`) and collapses Replogle performance (`v5 = 0.0602`); deployments must maintain `p_k ≥ 1.0`.
7. **Full Foundation-Model Fine-Tuning Compute & Architecture Constraints**: Full 12-layer `scGPT_human` fine-tuning (`~10.1 min/epoch/line` on Apple Silicon MPS without CUDA `flash-attn`, `~100h` across 20 splits) and Arc Institute `STATE` (`pert_rep=onehot` incompatible with unseen-gene union holdout without non-commercial `SE-600M` embeddings + NVIDIA CUDA cluster training) could not be completed on local workstation hardware and are transparently omitted rather than approximated.
