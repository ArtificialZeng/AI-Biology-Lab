## 2. Detailed Audit for Question 1: Has Anyone Reported Post-Amplitude Line-Specific Recovery of FCT-Class Methods?

### 2.1 Audit of Named Methods (`CellularAI` & `AtlasShift`)

1. **`Illumina / BioInsight CellularAI` (VCC 2026 1st Place, test overall `0.4399`)**
   - **Verified mechanism (`Illumina/CellularAI` repo + Illumina Nov 19, 2026 blog):** Combines summary-level log2 fold changes across training studies via DerSimonian–Laird random-effects meta-analysis + control-similarity weighting (`pooling/meta_analysis.py`), applies an SVD + Ridge control-conditioned residual adjustment on low-rank response coordinates (`prediction/residual.py:64-191`), scales pooled log2FC multiplicatively onto target control gene fractions `fc * 2^L` (`prediction/synthesis.py:45-47`) alongside jackknife variance-credit amplitude bisection (`prediction/totals.py:102-112`, `170-190`), and synthesizes 400 integer-count cells via quantile/copula matching.
   - **What is evaluated & reported (`docs/evaluation.md`, `evaluation/scoring.py`, Illumina release blog `Introducing the CellularAI algorithm`):**
     - `CellularAI` ships only the **`vcc2025` (`H1` hESC)** single-context evaluation harness covering the 6 standard competition metrics (`pds`, `expr_mse`, `fid`, `reach`, `jac`, `nmae`, averaged to `scaled.overall`).
     - In Illumina's Nov 19, 2026 technical blog, BioInsight reports leave-one-line-out validation on `H1`, `Replogle K562`, and `HEK293` solely via Arc-aligned overall scores (`"2X higher performance in comparison to legacy predictive standards (mean level of expression across measured cells)"`), notes that `"the algorithm also makes a small, conservative adjustment for how strongly the new cell type expresses each gene, since a gene that is barely expressed has little room to fall"`, and states that *"a full manuscript providing greater detail into the methods underlying this first version of the CellularAI algorithm will be released in early 2027."*
   - **Strict negative finding on Q1:** `CellularAI` does **not** isolate or report an FCT baseline, does **not** remove whole-line multiplicative amplitude ($\beta_t S$), and does **not** measure `r_orth_cal` or mismatched-control specificity on line-specific residuals.

2. **`AtlasShift` (`kaipengm2/Virtual-Cell-Challenge-2026`, VCC 2026 Public Leaderboard #82)**
   - **Verified mechanism (`README.md`, `model.py:112-130`, `predict.py:196-207`, `model.py:332-444`):** Transfers Dirichlet-shrunk, cross-perturbation-centered `log2fc` and `bulk_delta` effects from K562, HCT116, HEK293T, H1, and CD4 T cells onto target control fractions/probabilities with fixed scalar multipliers (`amplitude = 0.6` for `log2fc`, `0.3` for `bulk_delta`; `control_probability * 2^effect` and `expm1(...)`), applies a promoter-neighbor ceiling, and fits integer counts via dual-moment iterative tilting (`dual_moment_counts`).

## 3. Detailed Audit for Question 2: Has Anyone Pointed Out Multiplicative Whole-Line Amplitude Contamination in VCC's Line-Specific Slope or Interaction Terms?

Round 9's dev check (`PREREG_A.md` §0 & §3.2) demonstrated directly on `Replogle K562 <-> RPE1` that multiplying the unweighted shared source response by **just a single scalar per cell line (`Transfer_Mean_GlobalScale`: $\hat{c}_t \bar{\Delta}^{\mathrm{src}}$)** yields misleading raw line-specific residual correlations (`r_cal = +0.5618` on `K562`, `+0.3078` on `RPE1`), which collapse to **`r_orth_cal = 0.0000`** once per-perturbation amplitude along $\bar{\Delta}^{\mathrm{src}}_p$ is orthogonalized. Question 2 audits whether any of the five named papers or broader 24-month literature already identified this flaw in **VCC's line-specific slope** or **two-way ANOVA interaction terms**.

### 3.1 Named Five-Paper Audit

1. **`CGC` (`Huang et al. 2026`, `bioRxiv 10.64898/2026.09.30.755756`, `yongqih/cgc-counterfactual-resolution`) — VERDICT: `部分做过`**
   - **What `CGC` already did:** In `cgc_tcell_1d/decompose.py:60-78, 329-355, 592-596` (and `cgc_tcell_1b/benchmark.py:766-920`), Huang et al. formally distinguish between:
     - **Unweighted additive residual** $T - S$ (`dtrue2`, `additive_cosine`), which leaves any target-vs-source whole-context amplitude mismatch $(\beta_t - 1)S$ inside the residual, versus
     - **Globally orthogonal residual** $T_{\perp} = T - \beta_t S$ (`nt2`, `alpha_novel`, `cosine_novel`), where $\beta_t = \langle T, S\rangle / \|S\|^2$, explicitly factoring out Tier-1 whole-context multiplicative amplitude $\beta_t$ and diagnosing `CONTEXT_AMPLITUDE_COMPRESSION` (`AMPLITUDE_CGC`, $\alpha < 0.5\text{–}0.8$). In `tahoe_held_context_scaling/TAHOE_HELD_CONTEXT_SCALING_PROTOCOL.md:53-59`, `CGC` similarly tracks aligned amplitude $\alpha_{\mathrm{cross}}$ and energy ratio $\kappa_{\mathrm{cross}}$ alongside cosine alignment.
   - **Where `CGC` stops short (clear gap):**
     - `CGC` (`2026-10-01`) **predates** Arc's VCC 2026 Wrap-Up (`2026-11-19`) and never discusses or critiques VCC's `line-specific slope` (`0.02–0.13`).
     - Although `CGC` implements a factorial ANOVA variance decomposition helper (`cgc_tcell/conditional.py:24-50`, `_anova_components` for $P\times D$, $P\times S$, $P\times D\times S$), it **never** critiques `Molina & Zhang (2026)`'s additive two-way ANOVA ($0$ mentions of `molina` across all `CGC` source/markdown files) or points out that additive $\gamma_{c,p}$ conflates multiplicative whole-line ($\beta_t$) and gene-wise ($m_{t,g}$) amplitude heterogeneity with cell-line $\times$ perturbation interaction.
     - `CGC` stops strictly at Tier 1 (one scalar $\beta_t$ per held-out context) and never introduces Tier-2 gene-wise cross-perturbation OLS gain $m_{t,g}$.

2. **`AMM-SimWMag` (`Sun et al., Genes Jul 2026`, `10.3390/genes17070816`, `PMC13409800`) — VERDICT: `部分做过` (Tier-1 single-stimulus only)**
   - **What it did:** In PBMC single-stimulus (`IFN-β`) cross-cell-type prediction (`genes17070816_wrap.txt` §3–4), Sun et al. show that unscaled source transfer under-shoots strong-responder cell types (monocytes, dendritic cells) and over-shoots weak responders; multiplying shared delta by a leak-free per-cell-type scalar $\hat{s}_h$ regressed from baseline ISG expression lifts `R^2-DEG` from `0.810` to `0.877` (oracle `0.872`).
   - **Gap:** Evaluates a single perturbation across immune cell types (`0` hits for `slope`, `anova`, `interaction`, or `multiplicative`). Never addresses VCC line-specific slopes, multi-perturbation two-way ANOVA $\gamma_{c,p}$ confounding, or per-gene multiplicative slopes $m_{t,g}$.

3. **`Systema` (`Viñas Torné et al., Nat Biotechnol 2025/2026`, `10.1038/s41587-025-02777-8`, `PMC13271886`) — VERDICT: `没有找到` (Addresses additive systematic offset, not multiplicative amplitude)**
   - **What it actually critiques:** `Systema` demonstrates that shared **additive** systematic variation between perturbed and control populations (selection biases/batch/common stress shifts shared across perturbations in a dataset) inflates control-referenced Pearson($\Delta_{\mathrm{ctrl}}$) and MSE, motivating centroid-referenced metrics (`perturbed mean`, `matching mean`).
   - **Gap:** Full-text XML inspection (`systema_fulltext.txt`) confirms **`0` occurrences** of `multiplicative`, `amplitude`, `scalar`, `slope`, `line-specific`, `context-specific`, and `cell-type-specific`. It does **not** warn that VCC's line-specific slope or ANOVA interaction terms absorb multiplicative whole-line amplitude.

4. **`Miller et al. (2026)` (`Nat Biotechnol`, `10.1038/s41587-026-03307-w`, `2026-10-01`, `PMID:42823510`; preprint `10.1101/2025.10.20.683304`) — VERDICT: `没有找到`**
   - **What it actually critiques:** Audited across the entire OA Nature Biotechnology article (`miller_2026_full.txt`, 261 blocks) plus `arXiv:2506.22641` and follow-up `Dai (2026-11-04, 10.5281/zenodo.23928733)`:
     - Miller et al. trace baseline over-performance on standard within-line metrics to two artifacts: **(i) control bias** (citing `Systema`'s systematic control-vs-perturbed shift) and **(ii) signal dilution** (unweighted MSE/Pearson diluted across non-DEGs when median DEGs per perturbation is ~1–3), introducing DEG-weighted `WMSE`, `weighted R^2`, and `Dynamic Range Fraction` (`DRF`) calibrated between `mean baseline` and `interpolated duplicate`. (Follow-up **Qingyuan Dai, Nov 2026**, separately shows Miller's DEG-weighted `WMSE`/`R^2` heavily reward predicting direct on-target CRISPRi knockdown itself.)
   - **Gap:** In its own Discussion/Limitations (`miller_2026_full.txt:51`), Miller et al. explicitly state: *"First, we focused on the unseen genetic perturbation and unseen combination prediction tasks, **leaving the unseen context task (predicting responses in unobserved cell types, donors or conditions) unaddressed**."* Full-text scan confirms **`0` occurrences** of `multiplicative`, `amplitude`, `slope`, `line-specific`, `cell-line`, `ANOVA`, or `fold change`.

5. **`Molina & Zhang (2026)` (`bioRxiv 10.64898/2026.07.24.740459`, `xinyizhanglab/perturbation-decomposition`) — VERDICT: `没有找到`**
   - **What it actually does (`anova.py:45-48`, `reproduction/decomposition.py:8-65`):** Formulates strictly additive four-component ANOVA $\delta_{c,p} = \mu + \alpha_c + \beta_p + \gamma_{c,p}$ (`alpha`: cell-line template `27.85%`; `beta`: conserved `29.39%`; `gamma`: perturbation $\times$ line interaction `23.51%`; noise `19.25%`). While `reproduction/decomposition.py:53-65` supports optional scalar projection removal onto the **cell-line template vector** (`remove_template(..., method="project")`), neither the paper nor repository checks or notes that additive $\mu+\alpha_c+\beta_p$ leaves multiplicative whole-line ($\beta_t$) and gene-wise ($m_{t,g}$) amplitude scaling inside nominal "interaction" $\gamma_{c,p}$.

### 5.3 Structured Comparison: `arXiv:2608.00152v3` vs. BioXArena Round 9/10

1. **Key Similarities:**
   - Both adopt **frozen rules/models prior to held-out/external unblinding**, staged hypotheses separated from exploratory diagnostics, permutation/mismatched-alignment null controls, cluster/perturbation-level bootstrap intervals (avoiding unclustered $p$-value hacking), and mandatory negative-result reporting (`PARTIAL` / `未确认`).
   - Both find that **unadjusted magnitude or in-domain signal hides cross-context failure**: `2608.00152v3` exposes in-domain VCC success collapsing to negative external transfer + `n_cells` entanglement; Round 9 (`PREREG_A v4`) exposes raw residual `r_cal` inflation from 1-scalar whole-line amplitude (`GlobalScale`) collapsing to zero under `r_orth_cal` (`Groups A & B` both `未确认` on R9 confirmatory lines).
2. **Key Scientific Differences:**
   - **Prediction Problem (Diagnostic Readout vs. Zero-Shot Counterfactual):** `2608.00152v3` maps an **already-measured** post-perturbation transcriptome $\mathbf{x}_i$ or frozen Geneformer embedding $\mathbf{z}_g$ to a **scalar target-gene AD knockdown strength** $y_i$. BioXArena Round 9/10 observes **zero perturbed target-line cells** and predicts the **full multi-thousand-gene expression delta vector $\Delta_{t,p}$** (evaluated on non-target genes $g \neq g_p$, explicitly masking the perturbed gene itself so direct on-target knockdown strength cannot drive score) purely from source lines + target unperturbed control statistics ($\mu_t, C_t$).
   - **Focal Artifact Diagnosed:** `2608.00152v3` isolates **sampling-depth entanglement (`n_cells`)** inside un-subsampled AD magnitude endpoints; Round 9/10 isolates **multiplicative whole-line ($\beta_t$) & per-gene ($m_{t,g}$) amplitude confounding** inside cross-line residual slopes/correlations and additive 2-way ANOVA interaction terms (`r_cal` vs. `r_orth_cal`).
   - **Why `2608.00152v3`'s `n_cells` Finding Still Constrains Round 10 (`S7` Control):** Even though our prediction target $\Delta^{\mathrm{obs}}_{p,t}$ is a mean log1p difference (less mechanically inflated than the two-sample AD statistic $A^2_{b,g}$), two indirect `n_cells` pathways remain active in our pipeline: **(i)** `reproducible` filtering (`n_cells >= 25` & un-subsampled **`ad_counts >= 50`**) + `split-half` denoising $c_{\mathrm{ceil}}^{\perp}$ co-selects perturbations with stronger knockdown *and* larger cell counts (reducing pseudobulk sampling variance $\propto 1/n_{\mathrm{cells}}$), and **(ii)** surviving cell counts correlate biologically with perturbation fitness/lethality and on-target knockdown depth. Hence Round 10's pre-specified **`S7` sensitivity analysis** (`n_cells` tertile stratification + partial correlation controlling $\log(n_{\mathrm{cells}})$ and knockdown depth, alongside un-filtered `all_targets` S1) directly addresses the exact vulnerability exposed by `2608.00152v3`.
