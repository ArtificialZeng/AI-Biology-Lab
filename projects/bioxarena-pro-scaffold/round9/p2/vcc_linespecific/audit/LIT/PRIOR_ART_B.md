# PRIOR ART AUDIT B: Target-Control Variance & Expression Scaling of Cross-Cell-Line Perturbation Responses

- **Prepared by**: Agent A-T5 (Team A — Literature, Official Metrics & Competition Monitoring)
- **Date**: 2026-11-21 (`Asia/Shanghai`)
- **Trigger**: Master Coordinator (Claude) `PREREG_AUDIT_R2.md` §3 R16 (`tmvs_check.py` finding prior to PREREG v3 lock)
- **Target Method Under Audit (`tmvs` / `Transfer_Mean_VarScaled`)**:
  $$\hat{\Delta}^{\mathrm{tmvs}}_{p,t,g} = \bar{\Delta}^{\mathrm{src}}_{p,g} \cdot \sqrt{\frac{C_{t,gg}}{\mathrm{mean}_{s}\,C_{s,gg}}}$$
  where $\bar{\Delta}^{\mathrm{src}}_{p,g}$ is the mean perturbation response in source cell line(s) $s$, and $C_{t,gg}, C_{s,gg}$ are the diagonal (per-gene marginal) variances of unperturbed control cells in target line $t$ and source lines $s$. On Replogle K562↔RPE1 dev data (`PREREG_AUDIT_R2.md` §3), parameter-free zero-shot `tmvs` achieved `r_cal = +0.2198` (`95% CI [0.192, 0.254]`, slope `0.0737`) for K562←RPE1 and `+0.2145` (`[0.199, 0.229]`, slope `0.0267`) for RPE1←K562, exceeding both `Hybrid_ProjShared` (`0.05 / 0.11`) and count-space fold-change transfer `FCT` (`+0.1394 / +0.0903`, slopes `0.0462 / 0.0111`).

## 1. Executive Verdict & `ALERT_B` Determination

> **`ALERT_B` RAISED (PARTIAL PRIOR ART ON TARGET-CONTROL EXPRESSION SCALING; NO EXACT MATCH FOR ZERO-SHOT `tmvs` VARIANCE-RATIO SCALING).**
> Adverse prior-art findings are reported without attenuation per project protocol (`P2_RULES.md`):
> 1. **Target-Control Mean-Expression & Learned Per-Gene Scaling — ALREADY USED (`ALERT_B` Triggered for Generic Expression-Scaling & `FCT` Claims):** Two independent 2026 lines of prior work already multiplicatively scale transferred cross-line perturbation effects by target-line control expression properties:
>    - **VCC 2026 #1 `Illumina/CellularAI`** (`synthesis.py:45-47`, `plan.py:248-249`) and **VCC 2026 #82 `AtlasShift`** (`kaipengm2/Virtual-Cell-Challenge-2026/model.py:121-125`) multiplicatively apply pooled cross-line $\log_2\mathrm{FC}$ around **target control baseline fractions** ($f_{p,g} \propto f^{\mathrm{ctrl}}_{t,g} \cdot 2^{\bar{L}^{\mathrm{src}}_{p,g}}$, algebraically equivalent to `FCT`), and `CellularAI` further scales cross-line $|\log_2\mathrm{FC}|$ by the target Negative Binomial variance factor $\sqrt{1/\mu^{\mathrm{ctrl}}_{t,g} + \phi}$ during DEG power ranking (`select_calls` / `power_score`).
>    - **`CASTER` (2026)** (`CanPert/src/canpert/model.py:15-19, 84-86, 109`) explicitly implements a control-encoded per-gene multiplicative rescaling `(1 + g_scale(ctx)) * prior` on the cross-line `PerturbMean` prior (and explicitly tests baseline-expression-dependent scaling in positive controls, `results_text.py:208-211`), although this learned weight **fails in strict zero-shot transfer** (H2 not supported; worse than unscaled `PerturbMean`).
>    - **`AMM-SimWMag` (Sun et al., *Genes* 2026)** applies a per-cell-type scalar regressed from baseline ISG expression to the mean delta, plus coordinatewise $\sqrt{\hat{\sigma}^2_g / \sigma^2_{\mathrm{ctrl},h,g}}$ to **single-cell deviations around the predicted mean** (preserving cell dispersion without changing per-gene $\bar{\Delta}$).
> 2. **Exact Parameter-Free Zero-Shot Control-Variance Ratio Scaling (`tmvs`: $\bar{\Delta}^{\mathrm{src}}_{p,g}\sqrt{C_{t,gg}/\mathrm{mean}_s C_{s,gg}}$) — NOT FOUND IN PRIOR ART:** Across 24 months of bioRxiv, arXiv, PubMed, EuropePMC, Crossref, web/Scholar search, GitHub perturbation repositories, VCC 2025/2026 public solutions, `CASTER`, `CGC`, `Systema`, and `Molina & Zhang (2026)`, **no prior paper or public codebase scales the cross-line mean perturbation response vector $\bar{\Delta}^{\mathrm{src}}_{p,g}$ by the ratio of target-to-source unperturbed single-cell control gene standard deviations $\sqrt{C_{t,gg}/\mathrm{mean}_s C_{s,gg}}$**.
> 3. **PREREG v3 Boundary Implication:** Because target-control expression scaling (`FCT`, `CellularAI` `fc * 2^L` & $\sqrt{1/\mu+\phi}$, `CASTER` `(1+g_scale)*prior`) is prior art and parameter-free `tmvs` shows strong diagonal variance correlation (`r_cal ≈ +0.21–0.22`), PREREG v3 must explicitly treat **both `FCT` and `tmvs` as first-order marginal comparators/baselines** (not final biological discovery claims), and require any off-diagonal covariance-transport model (`CIPHER` $C_t C_s^{-1}$) to outperform `tmvs` directly in order to isolate off-diagonal gene-gene co-fluctuation wiring ($C_{t,gj}, g \neq j$) from marginal gene variability ($C_{t,gg}$).

## 3. Deep Technical Audits: CASTER, CGC, Systema & VCC 2025/2026 Solutions

### 3.1 `Illumina/CellularAI` (VCC 2026 1st Place — Closest Operational Relative)
Verified in `Illumina/CellularAI` (`docs/method.md`, `pooling/{weights.py, meta_analysis.py, catalog.py}`, `prediction/{build.py, plan.py, placement.py, residual.py, totals.py, synthesis.py}`):
1. **Cross-line pooling (`pooling/`):** Weights source cell lines over perturbations and genes using across-line perturbation `response_variance`, DerSimonian–Laird between-line heterogeneity $\sigma^2_{t,g} + \tau^2_g$ (`inv_mad_dl`), HVG Spearman similarity on `log1p(CPM)` control profiles (`sim_factor`), knockdown efficiency (`kd_factor`), and `noise_ratio`/`se_factor`. Nowhere does within-line single-cell control variance $C_{t,gg}$ enter pooling or scaling.
2. **Control-fraction scaling during generation (`synthesis.py:45-47`):** Given pooled $\log_2$ fold changes $L_{p,g}$, predicted fractions in target context $t$ are computed multiplicatively from the target line's unperturbed pooled gene fractions $f^{\mathrm{ctrl}}_{t,g}$ (`self.fc`):
   $$f^{\mathrm{pred}}_{p,t,g} = \frac{f^{\mathrm{ctrl}}_{t,g} \cdot 2^{\mathrm{clip}(L_{p,g})}}{\sum_{j} f^{\mathrm{ctrl}}_{t,j} \cdot 2^{\mathrm{clip}(L_{p,j})}}$$
   When transformed back to `log1p(TS * frac)` space (`totals.py`, `scoring.py:23-26`), this compresses perturbation shifts at low baseline counts $\mu^{\mathrm{ctrl}}_{t,g}$ identically to Team A's **`FCT` comparator**.
3. **Target NB dispersion weighting in DEG prioritization (`plan.py:248-249`, `placement.py:60-68`):** When scoring which genes will pass Wilcoxon DE calls, `select_calls` and `power_score` divide $|L_{p,g}|$ by the target-line Negative Binomial coefficient-of-variation proxy $\sqrt{1/\mu^{\mathrm{ctrl}}_{t,g} + \phi}$ (`mu = cpm * depth / 1e6`). Thus `CellularAI` *does* rescale transferred perturbation magnitudes by target control expression abundance $\mu^{\mathrm{ctrl}}_{t,g}$, both via count-space $f^{\mathrm{ctrl}} \cdot 2^L$ and NB $\sqrt{1/\mu + \phi}$, triggering `ALERT_B` for generic target-expression scaling claims, while remaining distinct from `tmvs`'s parameter-free single-cell variance ratio $\sqrt{C_{t,gg}/\mathrm{mean}_s C_{s,gg}}$.
4. **Clarification on `residual.py:94-116` (`control_scale`):** `CellularAI`'s `ControlResidual` standardizes regression features by `np.sqrt(variance)` **across training cell lines** of summed control `log1p(CPM)`, not within-line single-cell variance $C_{t,gg}$.

### 3.2 `CASTER` (`CanPert/src/canpert/`)
Audited in `model.py`, `methods_formal.py`, `theory_text.py`, `results_text.py`:
- **Architecture (`model.py:15-19, 84-86, 109`, `results_text.py:199-203`):** Encodes $S=32$ unperturbed control cells via self-attention + mean pooling to form `ctx` (`h_cell.mean(dim=1)`), adds FiLM conditioning (`perez2018`), and applies a zero-initialized per-gene multiplier term `pred = pred + ((1 + self.g_scale(ctx)) * prior)[:, None, :]` (plus a cell-resolved residual), trained across training lines via Energy-MMD + pseudobulk MSE so an untrained model equals unscaled `PerturbMean`.
- **Simulation vs. Empirical Zero-Shot Behavior (`results_text.py:204-212, 272-273, 384-406`):** CASTER's positive control (`results_text.py:208-211`) proves `(1 + g_scale(ctx))` **can** learn baseline-expression-dependent scaling when trained with supervision, and returns to `PerturbMean` under pure noise. However, in **strict zero-shot evaluation across Replogle lines (Task T2 / Hypothesis H2)**, **H2 was not supported: unscaled `PerturbMean` significantly outperformed `CASTER` on all three primary metrics**, because cross-line weights learned on source cell lines pulled predictions toward shared perturbation axes (Proposition 4). This provides sharp negative evidence against learned parametric `(1 + g(ctx))` generalization zero-shot, underscoring why parameter-free `tmvs` outperforming `Hybrid_ProjShared` (`0.22` vs `0.05/0.11`) needed careful mechanistic dissection.

### 3.3 `CGC` (`yongqih/cgc-counterfactual-resolution`)
Audited in `tcell_truth`, `tcell_lodo`, `held_context_scaling`, and `lea_archs4`:
- **`held_context_scaling` (`TAHOE_HELD_CONTEXT_SCALING_PROTOCOL.md`, `scaling.py`):** Evaluates zero-shot cross-context recovery across 50 Tahoe cell lines via cross-plate replicate energy $g = 1 - \sum\langle e_6, e_{14}\rangle / \sum\langle r_6, r_{14}\rangle$. Its unscaled shared-response reference is the unweighted mean across training contexts (`g = 0` by definition). Control profiles (DMSO wells from `Tahoe-0D`) are centered across genes and **L2-normalized** (`||x||_2 = 1`) before entering dual Linear or RBF context-kernel Ridge models.
- **Supervised Program Standardization (`programs/decompose.py:38-41`, `level2_full.py:21-29`):** Z-scores perturbation matrices by training-perturbation standard deviation (`axis=0` over perturbations, not cells) strictly for PCA/NMF program fitting and Ridge stability. CGC never rescales cross-line $\bar{\Delta}^{\mathrm{src}}$ by target control variance $C_{t,gg}$.

### 3.4 `Systema` (Viñas Torné et al., *Nat Biotechnol* 2026) & VCC 2025/2026 Landscape
- **`Systema` (`PMC13271886`):** Introduces `perturbed mean` (centroid of all perturbed cells in training set) and `matching mean` (for genetic combinations $X+Y$) on median-total-count-normalized, log-transformed top 5,000 HVGs to expose dataset-level systematic variation in reference-free metrics (centroid accuracy). Neither normalization nor baselines use per-gene target-control variance or expression scaling.
- **VCC 2025 & VCC 2026 Public Solutions:** Across `PerturbMean` (`ArcInstitute/state`), `PRiMeFlow` (`altoslabs/primeflow`, `arXiv:2604.13986v2`), `TransPert` commentary (`arXiv:2511.16954v1`) + follow-up (`Liu et al. 2026`, `perturbation-structure`), `Luan et al. 2026` (`10.64898/2026.10.20.760514`, source-centered ranks + isotonic regression), `Xaira X-Cell`, and `AtlasShift` (`kaipengm2/Virtual-Cell-Challenge-2026`), only `CellularAI` (`fc * 2^L` and NB $\sqrt{1/\mu+\phi}$) and `AtlasShift` (`control * 2^effect`) rescale transferred $\log_2\mathrm{FC}$ using target control **mean** expression; none use target single-cell control variance $C_{t,gg}$.

## 4. Does `tmvs` Conflict with Molina & Zhang (2026)? Exact Locus Analysis

**Short Answer:** **No direct empirical conflict on shared experimental tests, but a sharp structural & interpretive divergence between Molina & Zhang's *additive* ANOVA/feature regression and `tmvs`'s *multiplicative* gene-wise variance scaling.**

### 4.1 What Molina & Zhang (2026) Actually Tested (`10.64898/2026.07.24.740459` & `perturbation-decomposition-main`)
1. **Additive Two-Way ANOVA (`decomposition/anova.py:45-48`, `fig1f_anova_results.json`, $n=638$ shared perturbations):** Decomposes Replogle pseudobulk deltas additively as $\delta_{c,p} = \mu + \alpha_c + \beta_p + \gamma_{c,p}$, attributing variance shares: `template` ($\mu+\alpha_c$, `27.85%`), `conserved` ($\beta_p$, `29.39%`), `interaction` ($\gamma_{c,p}$, `23.51%`), and `noise` (`19.25%`).
2. **Template vs. Residual Projection onto Control PCs (`fig1e_ctrl_space_results.json`):** Tests linear subspace span of control cell PCs. The top 5 control PCs explain `72.3%–88.8%` of template variance ($\mu+\alpha_c$) vs only `7.4%–11.1%` of residual variance ($\beta_p+\gamma_{c,p}$); the top 100 control PCs explain `91.8%–98.3%` vs `34.6%–39.3%`.
3. **Target Control Features vs. DepMap (`fig4_supp_results.json`, `s4a_cross_features`):** Trains linear/MLP predictors from target-line mean control vector `ctrl_mean` alone (`Target ctrl` `pref`: **K562 `-0.045`, RPE1 `+0.083`, HepG2 `+0.041`, Jurkat `+0.001`**) vs `DepMap` alone (`0.096–0.239`) vs `DepMap + tgt ctrl` (`0.096–0.238`). Notice `Target ctrl` here means **additive regression of deltas onto $\mu^{\mathrm{ctrl}}_t$** without perturbation identity, and uses **control means** (no control gene variance $C_{t,gg}$, and no multiplicative interaction with $\bar{\Delta}^{\mathrm{src}}_{p,g}$).
4. **Cross-Line Source Transfer (`benchmarks/tahoe_drugs.py:124-183`):** Benchmarks unscaled `Direct` (`mean(source deltas)`) and target-trained PCA+Ridge (`Source_k`), never multiplicative control scaling.

### 4.2 Algebraic Origin of `tmvs` Signal vs. Molina's Interaction Term
Write the true response in target line $t$ (after template subtraction) as a conserved axis $\beta_{p,g}$ modulated by a line-by-gene marginal gain factor $m_{t,g}$ plus genuine perturbation-specific rewiring:
$$\Delta_{p,t,g} = m_{t,g}\,\beta_{p,g} + \gamma^{\mathrm{wire}}_{p,t,g}$$
Because Molina & Zhang's ANOVA subtracts the unweighted cross-line mean $\bar{\Delta}^{\mathrm{src}}_{p,g} = \bar{m}\,\beta_{p,g}$ additively, any line-by-gene marginal responsiveness heterogeneity $(m_{t,g} - \bar{m})\,\beta_{p,g}$ is automatically **folded into the additive interaction residual $\gamma_{c,p}$**:
$$r_{p,t,g} \equiv \Delta_{p,t,g} - \bar{\Delta}^{\mathrm{src}}_{p,g} = \underbrace{\big(m_{t,g} - \bar{m}\big)\,\beta_{p,g}}_{\text{rank-1 marginal scale residual}} + \gamma^{\mathrm{wire}}_{p,t,g}$$
Under `tmvs`, $\hat{r}^{\mathrm{tmvs}}_{p,t,g} = \big(\sqrt{C_{t,gg}/\mathrm{mean}_s C_{s,gg}} - 1\big)\,\bar{\Delta}^{\mathrm{src}}_{p,g}$. Thus:

1. **Non-Conflict Locus 1 (Control-Only Features vs. Control × Source Interaction):** Molina & Zhang tested whether target `ctrl_mean` *alone* (without interacting with $\bar{\Delta}^{\mathrm{src}}_{p,g}$) predicts perturbation responses/residuals (`pref ≈ 0`). Because $\mu^{\mathrm{ctrl}}_t$ carries no index $p$, it can only shift the global intercept/template ($\alpha_c$), not perturbation-specific variation across $p$. By contrast, `tmvs` multiplies a control-derived diagonal factor by the perturbation-indexed source response $\bar{\Delta}^{\mathrm{src}}_{p,g}$.
2. **Non-Conflict Locus 2 (Weak Calibration Slopes Respect the Small Variance Share):** Although `tmvs` obtains `r_cal = +0.2198` (K562←RPE1) and `+0.2145` (RPE1←K562), its linear calibration slopes on the true residual are very small (**`0.0737`** and **`0.0267`**). Hence `tmvs` recovers the **sign and relative ranking across genes of marginal dynamic-range / responsiveness scaling** (capturing genes silent or tightly constrained in K562 but transcriptionally plastic in RPE1), while leaving the overwhelming majority of residual amplitude and true perturbation-by-line rewiring ($\gamma^{\mathrm{wire}}_{p,t,g}$, e.g. TP53-dependent stress in RPE1) unexplained — fully consistent with Molina & Zhang's conclusion that most of $\gamma_{c,p}$ is not explained by baseline control state alone.
3. **Diagnostic Conflict Locus (Additive ANOVA Misattributes Transcriptional Competence / Dynamic-Range Scaling to "Wiring Interaction"):** Strictly speaking, $(m_{t,g} - \bar{m})\,\beta_{p,g}$ is **not** perturbation-specific regulatory rewiring — it reflects whether gene $g$ is expressed and transcriptionally responsive in target line $t$'s resting state (for instance, `log1p` zero-floor compression captured partially by `FCT` at `r_cal = +0.1394 / +0.0903`, plus continuous cell-to-cell fluctuation amplitude $C_{t,gg}$ lifting `r_cal` to `+0.2198 / +0.2145`). Team A's planned **R15 sensitivity `S5`** (excluding bottom-25% low-variance control genes) and direct comparison of `CIPHER` ($C_t C_s^{-1}$) against `tmvs` will cleanly decouple: (i) dynamic-range / active-gene gating (`FCT` / `S5`), (ii) marginal control dispersion scaling (`tmvs`, diagonal $C_{t,gg}$), and (iii) genuine off-diagonal co-fluctuation rewiring ($C_{t,gj}, g \neq j$).
