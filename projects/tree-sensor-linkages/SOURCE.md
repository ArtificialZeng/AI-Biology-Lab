# Primary sources and problem status — 26 October 2026

The precise target is the retained-root, single-input, multiple-calibrated-output tree model specified in [SCOPE.md](SCOPE.md). It is a newly formulated restricted question within a published broader research direction, rather than a named open conjecture.

- Meshkat and Shiu, *Structural identifiability of compartmental models: Recent progress and future directions*, [arXiv:2507.04496v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2507.04496v1), Questions 5.2 and 5.4: broader multiple-input/output extensions and minimal placements. These broad questions remain unresolved outside the stated hypotheses.
- Bortner, Gross, Meshkat, Shiu and Sullivant, *Identifiability of linear compartmental tree models and a general formula for input-output equations*, [arXiv:2106.08487v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2106.08487v2), Proposition 5.7; Advances in Applied Mathematics 146 (2023), 102490, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.aam.2023.102490). The root-only generic rank 2n−1 is known and is essential to the proof.
- Zernik, *Taylor Expansion Proof of the Matrix Tree Theorem — Part II*, [arXiv:1308.2160v1](https://arxiv.org/html/1308.2160v1), Theorem 2 and Definition 4: the classical signed all-minors forest identity used here. Classical attribution: Chaiken (1982), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1137/0603033); that publisher text was not directly inspected.

The [bounded comparison](LITERATURE.md) additionally covers existing general leak-placement results, a recent chain classification, and newer multiple-output work with an explicit source-access limitation. It does not certify first discovery. The earlier two-leak result in this lab is subsumed by the present arbitrary-leak result.
