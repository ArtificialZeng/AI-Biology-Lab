- Ahmed et al., [arXiv:2412.05283v3](https://arxiv.org/html/2412.05283v3), supply the coefficient formulas and local-identifiability classification.
- Meshkat and Shiu, [arXiv:2507.04496v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2507.04496v1), Question 5.3, identify the broader degree problem.
- Huneke and Keen's [2025 REU report](https://artsci.tamu.edu/mathematics/_files/_docs/reu/results/2025/huneke-keen-report.pdf), especially §5.1, studies earlier zero/one-leak degrees. Its [supporting code](https://github.com/tylerhuneke/linear-compartmental-models) is linked for comparison.

The proofs and two verification implementations in this directory were independently written with AI assistance. No third-party paper or author source code is redistributed.
