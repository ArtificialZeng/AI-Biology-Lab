# Frozen source record — 2026-10-26

## Assigned question

For leak-interlacing directed n-cycle models with one input, one output, and exactly two leaks, determine a generic global identifiability degree and an exact count of strictly positive parameter fibers in a natural infinite placement family. This is a new subsidiary question adjacent to the broad degree question below, not a claim to resolve all compartmental-model identifiability problems.

1. Saber Ahmed et al., *Identifiability of directed-cycle and catenary linear compartmental models*, arXiv:2412.05283v3, revised 2025-08-30; SIADS 25(1), 304–350 (2026), DOI 10.1137/25M1728636. https://arxiv.org/html/2412.05283v3 and https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.05283. Tyler Huneke and Tegan Keen, *On the Identifiability of Linear Compartmental Model Parameters*, Texas A&M REU report, 2025-07-17. https://artsci.tamu.edu/mathematics/_files/_docs/reu/results/2025/huneke-keen-report.pdf. The two-leak expression below has an extra factor p relative to that zero/one-leak expression and is proved by an explicit bijection. Local research-input PDF and extraction live under evidence/ and are not publication deliverables.
