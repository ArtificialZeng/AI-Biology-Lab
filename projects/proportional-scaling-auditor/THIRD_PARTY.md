# Source credit and rights

The source epidemic class, its main global-stability theorem and the proportional-scaling statement belong to David J. D. Earn and C. Connell McCluskey. McCluskey (2003) supplies the cited normalization comparison. Razvan and Yasaman (2012) already studied the broader two-infective model family and bistability; neither is a new contribution of this directory. See the [primary sources and comparison](sources.md).

This directory contains original explanations and independently written verification code. It redistributes no source paper PDF, author manuscript, third-party HTML, private correspondence or source figures. Its MIT license covers only the original additions in this directory; it does not relicense cited work or a containing upstream repository.
