# Primary sources and bounded comparison

**The model family and bistability are known. The contribution here is only a paper-specific correction to the unqualified proportional-scaling extension in Earn–McCluskey (2025). No new epidemic model, general dynamics theorem or biological bistability mechanism is claimed.**

## The statement corrected

David J. D. Earn and C. Connell McCluskey, *Global stability of epidemic models with uniform susceptibility*, PNAS **122**(49), e2510156122 (2025), [DOI and publication](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2510156122), [arXiv 2512.11003v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.11003v1). The inspected [author publication page](https://davidearn.mcmaster.ca/publications) links the [accepted author PDF](https://ms.mcmaster.ca/earn/pdfs/EarnMcCluskey2025_accepted.pdf). Its p. 3 permits arbitrary positive compartment mortalities and reducible transition matrices; p. 5 retains the unqualified proportional-scaling extension. The main constant-recruitment theorem is a separate statement and is untouched by this correction.

## Known two-infective family and bistability

Razvan and Yasaman, *Global analysis of an SAIS model*, Journal of Biological Dynamics **6**(2), 457–474 (2012), [DOI and publication](https://doi.org/10.1080/17513758.2011.621452), [publisher-provided full text](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/230636633_Global_analysis_of_an_SAIS_model). The fresh reviewer inspected Section 2, pp. 458–459, Eqs. (1)–(3), and Theorem 5.2, pp. 464–465. This paper already studies a broader two-infective family and the disease-free sink, endemic sink and saddle pattern. It receives credit for the model family and dynamical phenomenon.

The review independently checked an exact equation-level embedding of the present witness in that family, with parent-dependent recruitment: d=1/2, delta=1/2, epsilon=33/2, b_SS=23/10, b_AS=b_IS=34/5, alpha=beta=9/10, lambda_A=24 and lambda_I=1; recovery, transfers and vertical births are zero. These are the 2012 paper's parameter names, not the vector beta used in the present checker. The embedding establishes family overlap. Because several submodel parameters are zero, the paper's strictly-positive-parameter classification is not imported as a proof for this witness; its dynamics are verified directly here.

## The normalization reference cited in 2025

C. Connell McCluskey, *A model of HIV/AIDS with staged progression and amelioration*, Mathematical Biosciences **181** (2003), 1–16, [DOI and publication](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0025-5564(02)00149-9), [author-uploaded full text](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/11043935_A_model_of_HIVAIDS_with_staged_progression_and_amelioration). The fresh reviewer inspected Section 2, pp. 2–3, Eqs. (2.1)–(2.2), which is the calculation cited by the 2025 proportional-scaling paragraph. Its active population excludes the removed class A. Common background mortality cancels on normalization, but terminal removal leaves state-dependent quadratic dilution terms involving the terminal infected fraction. This supports retaining the composition-dependent dilution term; it does not justify unconditional formal equivalence for the arbitrary-mortality 2025 extension.
