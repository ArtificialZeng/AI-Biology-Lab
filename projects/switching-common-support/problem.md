# Exact frozen target

For the six reactions and fixed positive rate constants in model.json, and every
kappa>0, start from one B and no A or C in either environment. Prove that all
literal hypotheses of source Conjecture 43 hold but the molecular population
reaches zero at a finite time almost surely. This disproves the stated universal
conjecture. The environment is irreducible; the population chain is reducible.

The proof must condition on the full environment and the B conversion time,
justify the postconversion mean, use the environment occupation law correctly,
and turn conditional mean decay into finite-time extinction through absorption.
Unconditional mean decay is not assumed. Finite algebra checks alone cannot
establish these probability statements. No classification of strengthened
premises, no sharp repaired theorem, and no experimental discovery is asserted.
