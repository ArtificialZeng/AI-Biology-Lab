# Primary sources and relationship to this construction

1. Theo Gibbs, Yifan Zhang, Zachary R. Miller and James P. O'Dwyer (2022), *Stability criteria for the consumption and exchange of essential resources*, PLoS Computational Biology 18(9):e1010521. [Publisher article and supplement](https://journals.plos.org/ploscompbiol/article?id=10.1371/journal.pcbi.1010521), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pcbi.1010521). Published Eq. (1) defines the original two-pathway model; the analytical equal-abundance setting and S1 Appendix provide the local Jacobian. The selected conjecture is in Results immediately before Fig. 6: real, negative B eigenvalues are proposed to guarantee equilibrium stability. This packet supplies a counterexample to that conjecture, and does not claim to refute the distinct sufficient theorem with symmetry hypotheses.

2. Y. Liu, J. Hu, H. Lee and J. Gore (2025), *Complex Ecosystems Lose Stability When Resource Consumption Is Out of Niche*, Physical Review X 15:011003. [Publisher record](https://journals.aps.org/prx/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevX.15.011003), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevX.15.011003). Its model and Appendix A/C analyze growth-consumption mismatch with a diagonal resource derivative and additional structural conditions. The broader mechanism of destabilization from mismatch is prior work. Those hypotheses do not certify the generally nondiagonal recycled-resource derivative used here.

3. Oliver J. Meacock, Massimo Amicone and Sara Mitri (2026), *Emergent competition resolves the paradox of stable microbial mutualisms*, ecoEvoRxiv, version 2, updated 19 August 2026. [Primary version record](https://ecoevorxiv.org/repository/view/13431/), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.32942/X2MD57). Supplementary Note 1.3.2 (S17–S22), Methods 4.1.1 and Fig. S5 analyze full population/environment block spectra, reduced interaction spectra, and environmental damping. This is a close conceptual predecessor and takes priority for those general observations. Its product-Monod auxotroph model, overflow shunt and environmental dilution differ from the Gibbs construction. The inspected v2 does not provide this native Gibbs real-negative-B counterexample, exact positive inflow interval, or canonical two-consumer obstruction. That comparison is bounded, not proof of universal novelty.

4. Françoise Tisseur and Karl Meerbergen (2001), *The Quadratic Eigenvalue Problem*, SIAM Review 43(2):235–286. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1137/S0036144500381988). Quadratic matrix pencils and structure-dependent stability are established mathematics. The pencil used here does not constitute a new general theory.

[The original authors' repository](https://github.com/theogibbs/essential-stability-criteria) supplies provenance. Its inspected R implementation uses a simplified parameterization different from the published Eq. (1) two-pathway equations. This packet independently reconstructs the published equations and makes no software-bug or native-R-execution claim. Author code and third-party source texts are not included or relicensed.
