# Complete restricted classification

Exactly N=3 Fleming–Viot particles move on sites {0,1,2}, with q(i,j)=1 for i≠j. Killing rates k0,k1,k2 are arbitrary strictly positive numbers. A killed slot chooses one of the other two slots uniformly and copies its site and ancestral label; same-site copying is retained. Let Delta3=F02−F20 be the source-defined surviving-label current.

For every point in this entire positive three-parameter domain,

    sign(Delta3) = sign(−(k0−k1)(k0−k2)(k1−k2)).

Thus Delta3=0 exactly when at least two killing rates coincide. In particular, k0<k1<k2 gives positive circulation in orientation 0→2→1→0. This is a complete result for the specified finite model; it does not classify all population sizes or solve infinite-space nonexplosion. Historical priority and external human confirmation remain pending.

## Four particles: reversal with the killing order fixed

Under the same motion and copying convention with N=4, let k=(1,t,t+1), t>1. The exact current has numerator 2t(t−1)(3t²+13t+8)P(t) and a degree-27 denominator with positive coefficients. P has degree 19 and exactly one positive root rho, which is simple and satisfies 4769/446<rho<3133/293. Delta4 is negative for 1<t<rho, zero at rho, and positive for t>rho.

Among N≥2 in this fixed unit-rate triangle, four is therefore the minimum population admitting opposite nonzero currents at two killing vectors in the same strict ordering chamber. N2 is excluded by the source's balance theorem; N3 is excluded by the whole-domain result above. Analyticity and the nonzero path derivative at rho give a local regular zero surface at (1,rho,rho+1), away from the equal-rate planes, with both signs nearby.

The N4 theorem concerns this entire path and the local surface only. It does not classify all positive N4 killing vectors or show reversal at fixed rate ratios. General copying-rate dependence of ancestral direction is already present in the nearby Brownian work. The N4 extension belongs with this N3 result rather than a separate paper.
