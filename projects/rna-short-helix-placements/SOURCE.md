# Source question and established results

Ashutosh S. Jogalekar, *Designability of RNA Targets with Up to Two Length-2 Helices*, [arXiv:2608.25194v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.25194v1) (25 August 2026), §16.2 asks:

> which targets with three or more isolated stacks admit modulo-2 separated colorings;

[Primary full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.25194v1). The paper proves the motif-free, no-length-1, at-most-two-length-2 guarantee. It leaves placement criteria beyond two as future questions and credits the three-root-branch failure to the earlier Haleš family. That failure is not a novelty claim of this project. The source preprint reports that independent human expert review was pending.

The unlimited comparable-short corollary in this project uses the source's F/Q construction and its existing single-short-bearing-child joins. The separate three-helix iff classification is an explicit structural refinement; its priority remains unconfirmed.

Nearest prior primary result: Boury, Bulteau, and Ponty, [WABI 2024](https://doi.org/10.4230/LIPIcs.WABI.2024.19), §§4–7; [journal extension](https://doi.org/10.1186/s13015-025-00278-6). The finite-modulus decision algorithm, local transfers, and universal all-helix-length-at-least-3 guarantee are prior work. The implication from a proper separated coloring to unique design is due to [Haleš et al.](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00453-016-0196-x).

Other overlap inspected: Jedwab, Petrie, and Simon, TCS 833 (2020), 147–163, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tcs.2020.05.046), [arXiv:1709.08088v5](https://arxiv.org/abs/1709.08088v5). Definition 3.1 and Corollary 3.4 require unpaired vertices at maximum depth. Our comparable-short examples permit unpaired vertices at several depths, including the exterior. This comparison does not establish priority against all related work.

Sources were inspected on 26 October 2026. Third-party papers are linked rather than redistributed. The broad source question remains unresolved.
