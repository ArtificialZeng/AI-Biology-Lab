# Literature and contribution boundary

Primary sources and public version histories were inspected on 26 October 2026.

1. [Jogalekar, arXiv:2608.25194](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.25194): only v1 was listed, dated 25 August 2026. Section 16.2 asks about placement criteria with three or more isolated stacks. Sections 3.2–3.5 and 16.3 distinguish the prior algorithm, all-long theorem, at-most-two extension, and known three-root-branch obstruction. The F/Q states and local joins in §§5–8 already give the comparable-short result as a short corollary. No new parity device or general decision capability is claimed.
2. [Boury, Bulteau, and Ponty, WABI 2024](https://doi.org/10.4230/LIPIcs.WABI.2024.19), especially definitions, the all-long guarantee, and conclusion; [journal extension](https://doi.org/10.1186/s13015-025-00278-6). The fixed-modulus decision/construction procedure is established prior work. The present contribution concerns an explicit structural classification of the smallest three-stack family and a simple sufficient placement condition.
3. [Haleš et al., Algorithmica](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00453-016-0196-x): proper separated coloring implies unique design in the stated model; saturated degree at most four is already classified. The three-root-branch failure belongs to the prior example family identified by Jogalekar. A coloring-certificate failure does not imply undesignability.
4. [Jedwab, Petrie, and Simon, arXiv:1709.08088v5](https://arxiv.org/abs/1709.08088v5), Definition 3.1 and Corollary 3.4; [published article](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tcs.2020.05.046). Their P-unsaturated floral class requires unpaired vertices at maximum depth. Exterior and multilevel unpaired vertices in the present examples violate that prerequisite, so that named corollary does not cover this entire class. This is a comparison with a specific theorem, not a comprehensive novelty proof.

Follow-up searches included `RNA designability three length-2`, `RNA short helices nested`, `RNA designability isolated stacks path`, `RNA short helices comparable`, and `three isolated stacks classification`, as well as the source title with `nested`. No equivalent explicit three-helix iff statement was found in the inspected primary sources. The screen is bounded and cannot establish priority. The full placement question with arbitrary additional long helices and incomparable short helices remains unresolved.

## Public source repository

The source author identifies [rna-at-most-two-short-helices](https://github.com/ajogalekar/rna-at-most-two-short-helices) as the canonical artifact. On 26 October 2026 its default branch was `final-publication-release`, with inspected head `bf7541f36d03d935f44745d4fc0a2ffc5f5e20be` dated 25 August 2026. The inspected README covered the at-most-two theorem and the known three-root-branch example. No later placement classification was identified there. The new statements and Python checks in this project have not been Lean formalized; the source artifact's Lean verification does not transfer to them.
