# Scope and unsettled cases

Every root is uniform on the Klein four state group. Edge kernels are
symmetric group-based stochastic matrices with each nontrivial Fourier
eigenvalue strictly between zero and one. K2P identifies the C and T
eigenvalues in the explicitly supplied character table; JC identifies all
three. Individual entries may be zero unless positivity is explicitly stated.
Positive definiteness plus nonidentity alone is not substituted for this
strict spectral condition. No CTMC embeddability assertion is made.

1. **Rooted two-sink theorem.** All finite rooted binary ancestral DAGs with
   exactly two distinct exit sinks, no outside incoming edges, and all other
   vertices hidden. Inheritance probabilities may be any fixed strictly
   positive values. The exact sink-joint image is the positive-entry part of
   the specified family. Keeping arbitrary common conditional descendants
   therefore permits exact replacement by a two-edge rooted fork.
2. **Single-edge boundary counterexample.** One explicit lawful four-leaf
   root-trapping diamond, suppressed to quartet 12|34. A rational admissible
   K2P target, hence also a K3P target, cannot be reproduced by the diamond.
   Pair correlations identify the internal edge independently of how all
   original pendant, attachment and core parameters are retuned. The obstruction
   also covers the broader positive-definite nonidentity stochastic domain
   allowing nontrivial eigenvalues equal to one; the rooted theorem does not.

The second result refutes the universal single-edge equality on the literal
nonnegative-entry strict-spectrum domain and the broader positive-definite
nonidentity stochastic reading. It does not show that a point is
missing from the closure. The rooted result concerns the two-edge replacement,
not a claim that every semi-directed 2-sub-blob has two sink attachments.

The arbitrary-graph JC single-edge equality, all general transition-model or
root-distribution cases, and any strictly-positive/continuous-time Kimura
counterexample remain unclaimed. No empirical data were analyzed, no wet
experiment was performed, and no effect on a published phylogenetic inference
is asserted. Bounded source checks and AI review do not establish absolute
priority or human correctness confirmation.
