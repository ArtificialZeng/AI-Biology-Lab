# Sources and provenance

Author software: Emilio Venegas, [Mantis](https://github.com/EmilioVenegas/mantis-delta), MIT, pinned commit [`0423b4ce556e987a2367fd293ca8db8ae0479927`](https://github.com/EmilioVenegas/mantis-delta/commit/0423b4ce556e987a2367fd293ca8db8ae0479927). The candidate differs only by `upstream.patch`; no unrelated numerical implementation or test was changed. Snapshots are source import trees, not distributable wheels, so setuptools-scm does not need to infer a Git version.

Mathematical source: D. F. Anderson, G. Craciun and T. G. Kurtz (2010), *Product-Form Stationary Distributions for Deficiency Zero Chemical Reaction Networks*, Bulletin of Mathematical Biology, [DOI 10.1007/s11538-010-9517-4](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11538-010-9517-4), particularly Theorems 4.1–4.3 and section 4.3. [Author-hosted manuscript](https://people.math.wisc.edu/~craciun/PAPERS_NEW/Anderson_Craciun_Kurtz_ProdFormFinal_2010.pdf). The conditional product form is normalized on a closed irreducible class and is zero outside it. Linear conservation laws need not identify this class. The paper itself is not redistributed.

The full original source tree contained no AGENTS.md, CONTRIBUTING instructions, or separate AI contribution policy in the bounded check. The public current issue/PR lists were empty during that check. This establishes neither priority nor absence of private maintainer knowledge. The result is an engineering audit and candidate repair; no claim of a new mathematical open problem is made.
