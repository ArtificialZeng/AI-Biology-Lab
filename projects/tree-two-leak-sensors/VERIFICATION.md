# Verification and limitations

A separate AI mathematical review on 26 October 2026 accepted the frozen
generic local statement. It checked the source hypotheses, the root-only rank
dependency, the positive harmonic-similarity curve, the path-intersection
equivalence and the extra-output rank argument. The public proof explicitly
justifies generic coprimeness by full-row rank, density of the coefficient-map
image and the nonzero universal resultant.

An independently written standard-library checker uses `dR=R(dA)R` at rational
resolvent frequencies, rather than the researcher's Markov recurrences. A new
seven-vertex branched tree gives root rank 13, a good-sensor rank 14 with a
nonzero rational minor, and a vertex-only-intersection rank 13 with an explicit
positive ambiguity curve. Input/sensor/leak coincidences and all 164 placements
on the small-tree representatives were also tested.

The first checker covers all 3,464 placements through n=6. Its finite-field
ranks are exact lower bounds on generic rational rank. The proof supplies the
upper bound and the all-n classification. Initial degenerate evaluation points
were replaced during exploration; the final deterministic hash-derived points
and at most three attempts are recorded by the executable. This is not a claim
that one numerical point establishes a rank-deficiency theorem.

The review inspected the actual recent mammillary archive: its code contains
zero or one central leak with a peripheral input and other peripheral outputs.
That code does not establish the present arbitrary-tree, two-leak,
root-retained theorem. The full associated manuscript was not available, so a
bounded comparison cannot exclude broader overlap or unpublished work.

These are separate AI checks and exact computations, not external human peer
review, priority certification or author endorsement. No global uniqueness or
claim about every positive parameter point follows from the result.
