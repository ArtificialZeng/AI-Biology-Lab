# Credit and permissions

The prior one-input/one-output tree results are due to Bortner, Gross, Meshkat, Shiu and Sullivant, [arXiv:2106.08487v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2106.08487v2), published as [Advances in Applied Mathematics 146 (2023), 102490](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.aam.2023.102490). In particular, the root-only rank result is a dependency, not a new theorem here.

The broader research questions are Meshkat and Shiu, [arXiv:2507.04496v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2507.04496v1), Questions 5.2 and 5.4. The recent [mammillary reproduction package](https://doi.org/10.17632/6vjy4wjsr6.1) was inspected as a possible overlap; the observed archive covers a different star-model family. Its full accompanying manuscript was not obtained, so priority is not certified.

No third-party paper, archive, or source code is redistributed. New proofs and verification code were independently written with AI assistance and use the lab's MIT license. NumPy, NetworkX and SymPy retain their own licenses. The second checker needs only Python's standard library.
