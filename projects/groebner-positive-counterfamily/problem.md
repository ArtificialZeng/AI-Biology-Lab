# Precise question and contribution

Ferrari et al., arXiv:2501.12233v2, Section 1.2, conjecture that the steady-state ideal of a mass-action CRN satisfying its conservation-law and stoichiometric-indecomposability assumptions has a reduced lexicographic basis of the form

    {x_sigma(n)-g_n(t), ..., x_sigma(2)-g_2(t), g_1(t)}, t=x_sigma(1),

for some permutation of the original species variables. Section 4 leaves practical structural conditions for this native-coordinate property as a broader program. A complete characterization is not supplied here.

The current source already proves generic linear-coordinate shape under a nonsingular-Jacobian hypothesis and includes abstract radical polynomial examples lacking native normal form. There is also an earlier public community CRN counterclaim with a nonradical origin-only ideal. These results and claims are credited in source.md.

The new restricted diagnostic asks whether the listed network assumptions, together with a positive stable equilibrium and nonsingularity at every complex zero, can still fail native shape throughout an entire positive rate cone. The six-reaction family in model.json gives an affirmative obstruction: every positive rate choice has a globally stable positive equilibrium, four simple torus zeros and a radical ideal, while neither of the two species orders has the desired shape. Saturating away coordinate axes does not repair it.

An explicit mixed linear coordinate and its exceptional slopes illustrate the established generic repair. This agrees with the source's stronger linear-coordinate theorem. No first-disproof priority, new general shape lemma, failure of the published GRN/Wnt calculations, reaction-count minimality or measured biochemical pathway is asserted.

Evidence categories: proof.md contains the all-rate and dynamical argument; the scientific scripts provide exact symbolic identities and finite/topologically complete supporting checks. Computational agreement is distinct from independent human verification and historical novelty.
