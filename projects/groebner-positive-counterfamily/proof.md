# A positive regular counterfamily for native-coordinate shape

The source literal conjecture has a credited earlier public counterclaim. This is an admissible positive/regular all-rate family within the source structural-conditions refinement program. General projection-separation and shape-lemma arguments are credited as standard.

## Six reactions and all-positive parameters

Let a,b,c,d,p,q be arbitrary strictly positive real rate constants. Consider

    0 -> X (a), 0 -> Y (b),
    2X -> X (c), 2Y -> Y (d),
    2X -> X+Y (p), 2Y -> X+Y (q).

With deterministic mass-action monomials, the ODE is

    x'=a-Ax^2+qy^2, y'=b+px^2-By^2,
    A=c+p, B=d+q, Delta=AB-pq=cd+cq+pd>0.             (1)

Every reactant and product complex has molecularity at most two. Stoichiometric reaction columns are

    e1, e2, -e1, -e2, -e1+e2, e1-e2.                 (2)

Their span is two-dimensional, so the left kernel is zero: there is no nonzero linear conservation law to append. No column is zero. In any nontrivial partition into two stoichiometrically independent subnetworks, both subspaces would have positive dimension and their dimensions would sum to two; each would therefore be one-dimensional. But (2) contains three pairwise nonparallel direction classes. Two one-dimensional subspaces cannot cover these three classes. Thus no such partition exists. This is the source's reaction-partition indecomposability condition, not mere visual graph connectivity.

## The entire steady-state ideal

Work over the characteristic-zero coefficient field K generated by the chosen rates. Put

    u=(Ba+qb)/Delta>0, v=(pa+Ab)/Delta>0.

Invertibility of the constant coefficient matrix in (1) gives the ideal equality

    I=(a-Ax^2+qy^2, b+px^2-By^2)=(x^2-u,y^2-v).     (3)

The quotient has K-basis{1,x,y,xy}; the coprime square-free univariate factors have no repeated root, so over an algebraic closure the variety consists of exactly

    (epsilon sqrt(u), eta sqrt(v)), epsilon,eta in{+1,-1}.

All four points are distinct. Equivalently the Jacobian determinant of the original balance vector is

    det J=4 Delta x y,

which is nonzero at each of the four complex zeros. Thus the ideal is zero-dimensional and radical and the balances are algebraically independent. These conclusions hold throughout the full positive six-rate cone; no equality of rates is assumed.

Both lexicographic species orders have reduced basis consisting of x^2-u and y^2-v, ordered according to the chosen leading variable. It is a reduced Gröbner basis because the leading monomials x^2,y^2 are coprime, the polynomials are monic and their tails are constants. Neither order gives a polynomial linear in its leading species variable with polynomial dependence on the other species variable.

Intrinsically, the x-coordinate takes only two values on four points, and at either fixed x-value there are two different y-values. Hence no polynomial y-g(x) belongs to I. The identical argument with x,y exchanged rules out x-h(y). There are only two species permutations. This proves failure of the source native-coordinate shape even with all additional regularity and positive-state conditions.

Boundary saturation cannot remove the obstruction. In K[x,y]/I, x and y are units, with inverses x/u and y/v, respectively. Hence

    I:(xy)^infinity=I.                                (4)

The four solutions lie in the algebraic torus, although exactly one lies in the physically positive quadrant. This does not claim that choosing the positive branch is experimentally ambiguous, nor that a Gröbner basis cannot solve the system.

## Positive equilibrium and global stability

The unique nonnegative equilibrium is x*=sqrt(u)>0,y*=sqrt(v)>0. The Jacobian there has negative trace -2Ax*-2By* and positive determinant4Delta x*y*, so it is strictly Hurwitz. In fact it is globally asymptotically stable in the nonnegative quadrant.

To prove the stronger statement, let r=(x^2-u,y^2-v)^T and

    M=[[-A,q],[p,-B]], D=diag(p,q).

Then the vector field is Mr. The symmetric matrix

    D M=[[-pA,pq],[pq,-qB]]

is strictly negative definite: pA>0 and det(DM)=pq Delta>0. Define

    W(x,y)=p integral_{x*}^x(s^2-u)ds
          +q integral_{y*}^y(s^2-v)ds.                (5)

For x,y>=0 each integral is nonnegative and vanishes only at its positive equilibrium coordinate; W is positive definite relative to(x*,y*) and tends to infinity as x+y tends to infinity. Along solutions,

    W'=r^T D M r<0 except at(x*,y*).                  (6)

On x=0 the first component of the vector field is a+qy^2>0; on y=0 the second is b+px^2>0. Thus the closed nonnegative quadrant is forward invariant. Coercive W-sublevel sets intersected with that quadrant are compact and forward invariant. Local Lipschitzness of the polynomial vector field and boundedness give forward existence for all time. The strict Lyapunov/LaSalle argument on each such compact set gives convergence to the unique equilibrium and its Lyapunov stability. No planar no-cycle inference or unsupported high-dimensional Hopf argument is used.

At a=b=c=d=p=q=1, one has u=v=1, I=(x^2-1,y^2-1), x*=y*=1, and J=[[-4,2],[2,-4]], whose eigenvalues are-2,-6. This is an exact witness, not a proof of the all-rate result by sampling.

## Classical mixed-coordinate repair, explicitly separated

The general shape lemma already explains why a suitable generic linear combination can separate a finite reduced variety. For this family its bad choices can be displayed exactly. Let t=x+lambda y with lambda in K and

    lambda!=0, D0=u-lambda^2 v!=0.

The four t-values are distinct exactly under these two conditions. Their monic eliminant is

    g(t)=t^4-2(u+lambda^2 v)t^2+(u-lambda^2 v)^2.

Modulo I, one obtains

    x=[(3u+lambda^2 v)t-t^3]/(2D0),
    y=[t^3-(u+3lambda^2 v)t]/(2lambda D0).            (7)

For example, (t-x)^2=lambda^2v first gives2tx=t^2+D0, and g(t)=0 replaces1/t by[2(u+lambda^2v)t-t^3]/D0^2, yielding(7). Conversely, substituting(7) into x^2=u,y^2=v reduces both identities modulo g; t=x+lambda y also follows. Thus the mixed-coordinate quotient is K[t]/(g), of degree four with distinct roots. The excluded choices lambda=0 or lambda^2v=u identify at least two of the sign points. A rational lambda outside this finite exceptional set always exists.
