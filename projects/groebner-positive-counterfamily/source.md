# Source comparison and limits

Inspected 27 October 2026. The contribution is a source-specific CRN realization. A bounded search found no exact equivalent of this positive/radical all-rate family in the inspected passages or linked eight-file repository; historical priority is not established.

**Primary source.** Paola Ferrari, Sara Sommariva, Michele Piana, Federico Benvenuto and Matteo Varbaro, *When algebra twinks system biology: a conjecture on the structure of Gröbner bases in complex chemical reaction networks*, [arXiv:2501.12233v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2501.12233v2), revised 26 September 2025. Sections 1.1–1.2, Conjecture 1, Theorems 1–2, Example 1D, Section 1.4 specialization warning and Section 4 were inspected. The formal conjecture requires mass action, included conservation relations and no stoichiometrically independent reaction partition, and permits a species permutation. Its formal statement does not impose an eliminant degree bounded by species count. Theorem 2 explicitly permits generic linear coordinate changes. Example 1D already supplies a radical zero-dimensional abstract polynomial obstruction; it is not a new discovery here.

**Published version.** [Bollettino dell'Unione Matematica Italiana, DOI 10.1007/s40574-025-00515-1](https://doi.org/10.1007/s40574-025-00515-1), online 21 October 2025, volume 19:745–760 (2026). Current publisher preview verifies metadata and strengthened-hypothesis abstract. Its full text is subscription content; the author-institution copy could not be retrieved. Final published Conjecture 1/Theorem 2 equivalence to arXiv v2 is unverified, so exact mathematical attribution targets the inspected current author preprint.

**Author repository.** [theMIDAgroup/CRN_Groebner](https://github.com/theMIDAgroup/CRN_Groebner), directly linked in the paper, at commit `03299837e4f037116c8aac56e6b86aa8f2c1bf10`. All seven Macaulay2 examples and README were read. Fully paginated issue, PR and comment histories were empty at inspection. The nearest two-species example has a conservation equation and one kinetic parameter; it is different from this open six-rate family. No source file from that repository is copied into this packet.

**Classical algebra.** David A. Cox and Carlos D'Andrea, *Subresultants and the Shape Lemma*, [arXiv:2112.10306v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2112.10306v2), Section 2, treats the established coordinate-separation/local-condition mechanism. The source paper also cites standard shape-lemma references. The projection collision argument, generic primitive-element principle and mixed-coordinate existence are credited as standard, rather than new general results.

**Prior public counterclaim.** A 27 August 2026 [community submission](https://mathdb.com/p/366733/triangular-grobner-basis-conjecture-for-chemical-reaction-ne) advertises an origin-only nonradical CRN counterexample. It is credited as an existing public claim; it is not treated as a certified primary-paper resolution or reproduced as this project's discovery. This prior claim and the source's abstract polynomial examples rule out any first-disproof or first-obstruction assertion here. The diagnostic increment is all-positive-rate kinetic admissibility, regular torus zeros and a globally stable physical equilibrium in one explicit family.
