# Exact claim under audit

There exists a rational strictly positive symmetric row-stochastic 4×4 matrix M, reversible for the uniform strictly positive distribution and with spectrum 1, 2^-9, 2^-27, 2^-27, for which:

- M=exp(Q) for a real zero-row-sum Q whose off-diagonal entries are strictly positive;
- the principal real logarithm Log(M) is not a Markov generator because Log(M)[1,4]=−3log(2)/4<0.

The literal matrix, Q and exact exponential decomposition are displayed in proof.md. This contradicts the stated arbitrary-embeddability⇒principal-generator implication in arXiv:2511.22800v1, Theorem 3.16, at its specified hypotheses. It does not dispute the known uniqueness/criterion when the generator itself is required to be reversible. General nonprincipal embeddings are known; historical priority of this particular correction is not established.
