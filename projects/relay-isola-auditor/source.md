# Source and frozen scope

Rim Adenane, Florin Avram and Andrei-Dan Halanay, *Persistence, Coexistence, and Boundary Transcritical Relays for Multi-Strain Epidemic Models*, Mathematics 14(18), 3271, published 9 September 2026. [Published article](https://www.mdpi.com/2227-7390/14/18/3271), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.3390/math14183271). Target: §6.3, Conjecture 2 and Remark 14. Retrieved 26 October 2026.

Its core conclusion states:

> Then, every connected component of the set of strictly positive equilibria in state–parameter space has a closure intersecting a boundary equilibrium branch

The original continues with a stronger finite-chain condition: nested siphon faces connected at invasion thresholds R=1, with the local nondegeneracy conditions of Theorem 7. It also states a weaker version asking only for intersection with the boundary relay graph and explicitly identifies a disconnected positive-equilibrium isola as a falsifier.

Exact mathematical reading: for a given smooth family of chemical ODEs, assume a finite lattice of forward-invariant siphon faces, isolated equilibria except at ordinary local bifurcation values, and that every codimension-one boundary hyperbolicity loss comes from a simple transversal Metzler eigenvalue. Then every connected component of positive equilibria must have the asserted boundary origin. The text does not require conservation, no inflows, bimolecularity, at least two strains, an actual boundary crossing, or independent variation of all kinetic constants.

Our counterexample uses one ordinary positive kinetic control, the inflow lambda, on its whole domain (0,infinity). The boundary-loss premise is satisfied because the boundary never loses hyperbolicity. The family contains a unique minimal siphon and a complete positive equilibrium isola. **No counterexample is claimed if the conjecture is instead strengthened to require the full space of independently variable rate constants.** The explicit path in proof.md shows why that distinction is necessary for this very network.
