# Prior credit and limits

- [Franco–Kepka–Velázquez2026](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00205-026-02183-7),
  Section8, is the exact audit target. Proposition2.2 already gives reversible
  diagonal symmetrization. The main cut-vertex theorem is outside this note.
- [Franco–Kepka–Velázquez2025](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00285-025-02191-3),
  Theorem3.2, uses real exponential expansions for response/renewal kernels with
  one entrance per compartment. Those kernels have different assumptions
  from arbitrary same-input cross-transition curves, but the spectral method
  is established prior work.
- [Qian–Elson2004](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.0305962101), text afterEq4,
  Eq5 and Eqs6–7/Fig3, gives equilibrium real/nonpositive spectrum, reciprocal
  transition/cross-correlation symmetry and flux information. Its four-state
  cross-correlations pair forward and reverse observations. Neither the real
  spectrum nor its use in nonequilibrium analysis is new here.
- [Amann–Schmiedl–Seifert2010](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.3294567),
  [primary preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/1001.0528), pp1–2/Eqs9–15, studies
  nonequilibrium evidence under partial observation and explicitly credits
  oscillatory relaxation as an already-known sufficient signature. Its
  coarse on/off observable differs from the Section8 response pair.

The bounded current-paper, author-page and correction searches did not locate
an equivalent published correction of these Section8 computations. This is
not proof of absence or priority. Any interpretation limited to local equality
constraints on finite jets must be distinguished from the literal unrestricted
full-curve wording. The positive determinant repair deliberately preserves the
valid narrower intention rather than inferring a broad failure from a
degenerate derivative point. This is a modest technical audit, not a new
general physical or biological phenomenon.
