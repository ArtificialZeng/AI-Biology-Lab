# Primary source and precise contract

Eugenia Franco, Bernhard Kepka and Juan J. L. Velázquez, *Characterizing the
Detailed Balance Property by Means of Measurements*, Archive for Rational
Mechanics and Analysis250:25(2026), published3April2026.
[Version of record](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00205-026-02183-7);
[arXiv2402.12935v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.12935v1).

Section8 considers complete four-state ergodic column Markov generators and
the displayed forward responses e2^T exp(tA)e1 and e3^T exp(tA)e1. It associates
them with six derivatives of orders1,2,3, and uses a six-coordinate symmetric
rate calculation at A0=J-4I to argue local openness. Its broader prose says
these nonreciprocal curves do not impose restrictions useful for detecting
detailed balance. The present note separates those mathematical readings.

The six symmetric off-diagonal coordinates include their induced diagonal
changes, as required by zero column sums. They are a restriction of, rather
than the entirety of, the nine-dimensional reversible family. No surrounding
real-spectrum/pole assumption was identified in the inspected definitions
or Section8. The source's Proposition2.2 already supplies reversible diagonal
similarity; this is prior spectral background.
