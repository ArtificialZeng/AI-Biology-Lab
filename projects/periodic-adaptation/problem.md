# Frozen mathematical scope

For a,b,c,d,T>0 and u:R->(0,infinity) continuous and T-periodic, consider
x'=(a+c)u-bx-dxy, y'=cu-dxy on the nonnegative quadrant. Prove unique positive
T-periodic response and global exponential attraction, with constants allowed
to depend on the initial state. On that response, prove positivity of
z=x-(a+c)(y-y0)/c, y0=bc/(ad), and the exact mean-deficit identity in README.
Classify equality and quantify the leading loss for a small sinusoid.

The original broad source periodic-input question remains unresolved. This
natural source-example extension is a result-note scope. It does not cover
all adapting networks, zero-valued/discontinuous signals, or finite-amplitude
frequency optimization. Mean depression relative to the constant-input
baseline does not by itself establish dependence on input average alone.

The new claim concerns the exact signed identity for this reversible motif;
known adaptation motifs, generic mean shifts, and contraction theory are
credited. A bounded source search is not a certificate of priority.
