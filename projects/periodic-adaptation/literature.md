# Prior results and limits of the comparison

- [Franco–Velázquez2026](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00285-026-02344-y): source
  Example3.8 supplies the reversible adapting motif. Section5 motivates the
  periodic question. Its constant-input result and completion are credited.
- [Krishnan–Floros2019](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12918-019-0703-1): adapting
  circuits can fail to preserve periodic output means; other motifs have
  exact conserved averages. That phenomenon and basic averaging are not new.
- [Iglesias–Shi2014](https://doi.org/10.1049/iet-syb.2014.0026): temporal and
  stochastic responses of adaptation motifs are established background.
- [Al-Radhawi–Angeli–Sontag2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.13678v4), and
  Margaliot–Sontag–Tuller, *Contraction after small transients*, Automatica67
  (2016)178–184: general contraction and entrainment methods are prior work.
  The explicit weighted-norm proof here is a specialization, not new theory.
- [Wafi–de Oliveira–Sontag2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.01573v3): a known
  triangular Sniffer model appears in AppendixB.5; its cumulative-response
  analysis concerns constant doses. The motif and integral readouts are credited.
- [Massas–Margaliot2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.15214v1): exact mean
  decompositions and strict losses under oscillatory forcing already exist for
  nonlinear cascades with linear-loss components. Generic signed mean shifts
  and decomposition methods are not claimed new here.
- [Rahi et al.2017](https://doi.org/10.1038/nmeth.4408): periodic stimulation
  distinguishes adapting circuits. The main article was inspected; its complete
  mathematical supplement was not verified in this bounded comparison.

The triangular incoherent-feedforward analogue admits the same style of
elimination identity. Thus novelty cannot rest on the Sniffer circuit or on
observing a mean shift. No exact subsuming theorem for this source example's
all-waveform signed identity was identified in the inspected sources, but
unread sources and equivalent formulations remain possible. Priority is
unverified; specialist assessment is requested. There is no newly observed
biological mechanism or experimental validation.
