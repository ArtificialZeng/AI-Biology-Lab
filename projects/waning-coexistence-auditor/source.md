# Sources and scope

Literature was checked on 26 October 2026. This is a bounded primary-source comparison, not a guarantee of priority.

The target is Rim Adenane, Florin Avram and Andrei-Dan Halanay, [*Persistence, Coexistence, and Boundary Transcritical Relays for Multi-Strain Epidemic Models*](https://doi.org/10.3390/math14183271), Mathematics 14(18), 3271 (9 September 2026). The published PDF was inspected directly: Eq. (6), pp. 15–16; Theorem 3, pp. 18–21; A1–A5 and Conjecture 1, pp. 23–25; Lemmas 6–8, Theorem 5 and Question 2, pp. 26–31. Its PDF SHA-256 is `a7dd157a271ee080961ad52d7007e099f9c66423fd43990472e8cab73d7164b5` (679,644 bytes). The paper is linked, not redistributed.

The model here is the full eight-compartment Eq. (6), with both secondary-infection directions, arbitrary positive ADE parameters and all three nonnegative waning rates. The source's Theorem 5 assumes an additional root in the positive reconstruction interval. The accompanying proof supplies that root from ordered invasion at all finite positive parameters. It also proves the converse when the second resident's DFE threshold is at least one. The permanent-immunity sufficiency subcase is already covered by the source's Theorem 3.

Precisely, all `Lambda, mu, beta_i, gamma_i, eta_i, sigma_i` are finite and strictly positive; `theta1, theta2, theta12` are finite and nonnegative. Set `p=(gamma1+mu)/beta1 < q=(gamma2+mu)/beta2` and `s0=Lambda/mu`. Ordered invasion `R2(E1)>1` suffices for a strictly positive coexistence equilibrium. If also `q<=s0`, it is necessary. The strain-exchanged ordering follows by relabeling. Equal thresholds are outside this statement. No uniqueness, stability or persistence conclusion follows. Broad abstract Conjecture 1 and formal Question 2's root-count and multiple-root classification remain unresolved.

## Closest prior results

- Nir Gavish, [*Revisiting the exclusion principle in epidemiology at its ultimate limit*, arXiv:2405.09813v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.09813v1) (16 May 2024). The full arXiv text and Theorems 2.1–2.2 were inspected. The full ADE/waning model is studied in a sufficiently large transmission-rate limit, with a stronger limiting invasion condition. The journal version is [*Revisiting the exclusion principle in epidemiology at the limit of a large competitive advantage*](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jtbi.2025.112045), Journal of Theoretical Biology 600, 112045 (2025). Its published metadata and abstract were checked; final full-text access returned HTTP 403. Detailed theorem comparison is therefore to the identified arXiv version, and overlap with an uninspected final revision remains a limitation.
- Gavish, [*A new oscillatory regime in two-strain epidemic models with partial cross-immunity*, arXiv:2412.07536v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.07536v1) (10 December 2024). Its full model and Theorem 2 already establish existence and uniqueness in the mutual-invasion region with `eta1=eta2=1` and permanent immunity. No novelty is claimed for that subcase.
- Gavish, [*Hidden Oscillations: How Minimal Immunological Interactions Destabilize Two-Strain Epidemic Models*](https://doi.org/10.1137/25M1826160), SIAM Journal on Life Sciences 1(3), 420–443 (2026). The full publisher HTML, model (1), assumptions (4), and Theorem 3.3 were inspected. This rigorous no-ADE, permanent-immunity family is credited as prior work.
- Gavish and Rabiu, [arXiv:2311.11318v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.11318v2) (21 August 2026), is explicitly **withdrawn**, because its analysis used unfulfilled asymptotic assumptions. It points to the simpler SIAM model above. It is historical context, not a valid full-model theorem.
- Gavish, [*An Analytically Tractable Framework for Multi-Strain Epidemics*, arXiv:2608.13995v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.13995v1) (14 August 2026). Full primary HTML was inspected in the fresh review. Section 6 and Proposition 5's analytic waning/quarantine extension retain `sigma21=0`; Section 7 explores positive `sigma21` numerically. This differs from the present all-positive bidirectional secondary-infection family.
- Ferguson, Anderson and Gupta, [PNAS 96, 790–794 (1999)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC15215/), gives classical ADE/coexistence context, including below-one DFE reproduction. The rational delimiter in the accompanying proof does not introduce a new ADE mechanism.

The source model repository is [florinav/EpidCRNmodels](https://github.com/florinav/EpidCRNmodels). No equivalently scoped theorem was found in the inspected primary sources; this coverage statement does not establish priority.
