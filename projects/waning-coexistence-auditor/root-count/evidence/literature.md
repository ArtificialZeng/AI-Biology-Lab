# Root-count literature refresh

Bounded primary-source check, 26 October 2026. This record supports scope comparison, not a priority guarantee. The earlier all-finite-parameter existence and `q<=s0` conditional converse are prior results; they are not new results of this root-count note. The formal target is recorded separately in [source-reading.md](source-reading.md).

- **Full ADE/waning, highly transmissible limit:** [Gavish, arXiv:2405.09813v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2405.09813v1), current official page still lists only v1 (16 May 2024). Full HTML Theorem 2.2 and Proposition C.1 explicitly require sufficiently large `beta1`, together with the limiting invasion inequality. They state existence and uniqueness in that regime, including positive secondary infection in both directions. A new theorem cannot claim that asymptotic uniqueness as new. The [JTB 600,112045 (2025) journal revision](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jtbi.2025.112045) was identified, but the final text remains inaccessible through the tool (redirect failure this pass; earlier fresh review reported 403). Detailed comparison is to the specified arXiv theorem, not an uninspected final revision.

- **Permanent immunity without infectivity ADE:** [Gavish, arXiv:2412.07536v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2412.07536v1), current official page lists only v1 (10 December 2024). Proposition 1 already gives a scalar cubic; Theorem 2 gives unique feasible coexistence in its mutual-invasion region. The model fixes secondary infectivity to one and has no waning. Thus the same parameter subcase, and cubic reduction by itself there, are prior results. Its coefficient signs should not be imported blindly: the final SIAM formulation below changes displayed signs and provides the current rigorous statement.

- **Current rigorous minimal-model result:** [Gavish, Hidden Oscillations, SIAM J. Life Sci. 1(3),420–443 (2026)](https://epubs.siam.org/doi/10.1137/25M1826160). Full publisher HTML inspected: model (1) has `eta1=eta2=1`, no waning, and cross susceptibilities in `(0,1]`. Proposition 3.1 is a cubic reduction; Lemma 3.2 classifies its feasible root in the stated mutual-invasion region; Theorem 3.3 states unique coexistence there and none outside. This is direct prior uniqueness in that subcase, not full positive ADE/arbitrary-waning classification.

- **Zero one-way secondary infection with waning:** [Gavish, arXiv:2608.13995v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.13995v1), current official page lists only v1 (14 August 2026). Section 6 retains `sigma21=0`. Proposition 5 gives a unique, explicit coexistence equilibrium for the waning/quarantine extension in its stated region. Section 7 studies robustness beyond this family numerically. A theorem for both strictly positive cross-susceptibility directions would extend the analytic scope; the zero-direction uniqueness is already known.

- **Withdrawn earlier waning analysis:** [Gavish–Rabiu, arXiv:2311.11318v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.11318), 21 August 2026. The official page explicitly withdraws the paper because its analysis relies on unfulfilled asymptotic assumptions and points to the simpler SIAM model. It supplies historical context, not a valid uniqueness theorem.

- **Nearby distinct waning model:** [Strain coexistence and competition for pathogens with asymmetric cross-immunity and waning immunity, medRxiv 2026.08.03.26359614v1](https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.08.03.26359614v1.full). The inspected primary full text derives a unique coexistence solution by a two-by-two linear system in a status-based model. Its four-variable equilibrium and susceptibility/history assumptions differ from the present eight-compartment model with independent secondary-infectivity ADE. Do not advertise coexistence uniqueness with waning generally as a new biological phenomenon.
