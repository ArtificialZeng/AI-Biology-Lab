# Independent held-out attack, pass 2

Twenty-four exact rational parameter vectors and population levels were checked against the proposed root-count table. Sixteen cases use four independently chosen base parameter vectors with four population levels each, selecting rational feasible poles and solving the ADE-product constraint; five supplied edge cases cover an interior double root, its two neighboring population levels, two prescribed rational equilibria, and the critical equal-threshold corner. An additional independently solved endpoint-tangent case uses theta1=54/11 and Lambda=93/55 (the other parameters of the supplied fold family), giving a=b=0.

Every case matched the table. The checker constructs the original population numerator directly, isolates every real root with exact rational intervals and multiplicities, then uses Sturm counts of Delta to separate those intervals from its poles. It never counts a boundary root at s=p as a strictly positive equilibrium. Exact algebraic signs evaluate the transformed coefficients; the Mobius coefficient identity is separately checked. No floating-point root estimate is used.

Coverage includes zero and nonzero waning, asymmetric positive rates, positive cross-susceptibility above and below one, strict and equal resident thresholds, a linear Delta, negative Delta quadratic coefficient, ordered-invasion and noninvasion endpoints, interior double roots, two simple feasible roots, and endpoint a=b=0. In the negative-leading-Delta case its second positive root lies beyond p: d is the unique root in (0,p), not necessarily the only positive root globally.

The normal and Python -O runs produced byte-identical JSON. The script emits stdout only; certificate creation used explicit shell redirection. These finite exact checks support an adversarial test of the frozen statement; they do not prove the infinite parameter theorem, novelty, stability or persistence, and are not a formal or fresh-referee acceptance.

Two subsequent supplied degree-collapse cases were independently reconstructed by the same formulas: mu=gamma1=gamma2=1, beta1=2, beta2=1, eta1=1, eta2=2, sigma1=sigma2=1 and all theta=1 give Delta=-s^2+6s-4, with d=3-sqrt(5). At Lambda=3 the exact numerator is (s^2-34s+32)/2, with one simple feasible root. At Lambda=5/2 it is 14(1-s), with no feasible root: the root s=p=1 is excluded. Thus P-degrees 1, 2 and 3 are all represented. The refreshed normal/-O certificates remain byte-identical.
