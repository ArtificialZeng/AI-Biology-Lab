# Independent bounded adjacent-port prior check

Date: 2026-10-26. Task: test immediate literature/standard-theorem overlap for the positive coefficient-Jacobian exceptional set of the bidirected path with input1/output2 and one fixed positive leak. This is a bounded screening result, not a priority certificate.

## Inspected primary sources

1. Gerberding, Obatake and Shiu, *Identifiability of Linear Compartmental Models: The Effect of Moving Inputs, Outputs, and Leaks*, https://arxiv.org/html/1910.13549 (published Linear and Multilinear Algebra70(14),2782–2803,2022). Theorem3.2 allows a single arbitrary leak for catenary models, but explicitly assumes input and output both at vertex1. Its assertion is generic local identifiability. Theorem3.5 permits moved input/output only for directed cycles. Neither statement supplies the noncollocated adjacent-port exceptional equation. Proposition2.4 supplies the determinant/minor input-output equation, and coefficient-map rank is a generic criterion.

2. Ferudun, *An Exact Singular-Locus Formula for Rooted-Tree Compartmental Models*,15October2026, unrefereed AI-assisted preprint, https://eulersolve.org/papers/owr-11695865-007/ and linked six-page PDF https://eulersolve.org/papers/owr-11695865-007/paper.pdf?v=e14b070a2aef . Abstract, setup and Theorem1.1 all fix the sole input, output and leak at the same root. Formula(4) is a monomial times squared sibling-branch resultants. This does not state the present input1/output2 exceptional factors. The word “adjacent” in its scope note refers to the adjacent conjectures GMS5.5/5.9 rather than adjacent physical input/output ports.

3. Ahmed etal., *Identifiability of Directed-Cycle and Catenary Linear Compartmental Models*, SIADS25(1),304–350,2026, DOI10.1137/25M1728636, https://arxiv.org/html/2412.05283v3 and publisher full-text link https://epubs.siam.org/eprint/IXIEMIECP3TISXXACWXU/full . Section5 says singular loci of generically identifiable catenary models remain poorly understood; its Theorem5.1 gives the coefficient formula. Corollary5.3 of Bortner etal.2023, cited there, gives generic local identifiability for adjacent ports and at most one leak, rather than exceptional equations. This supports a research direction but does not certify this exact target is open.

4. Bortner etal., *Identifiability of linear compartmental tree models and a general formula for input-output equations*, Adv.Appl.Math.146(2023)102490, primary PDF https://par.nsf.gov/servlets/purl/10401019 . Inspected source text at Corollary5.3 and Lemmas5.5/5.6: complete generic one-input/one-output classification; no adjacent-port positive exceptional formula in these statements.

5. Chen, Landaw and DiStefano, *Algorithms for the identifiable parameter combinations and parameter bounds of unidentifiable catenary compartmental models*, Math.Biosci.76(1)(1985),59–68, DOI10.1016/0025-5564(85)90046-X, https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/002555648590046X . Primary abstract explicitly concerns input and output both in the first pool. The paper is relevant precedent for endpoint inverse algorithms, but the inspected abstract does not establish an equivalent noncollocated equation. Full text was not inspected.

Full theorem-level text was unavailable in this screening. Historical overlap remains unresolved rather than excluded.

7. Teschl, *Jacobi Operators and Completely Integrable Nonlinear Lattices*, AMS Mathematical Surveys and Monographs72(2000), author site https://radon.mat.univie.ac.at/~gerald/ftp/book-jac/index.html and author PDF https://radon.mat.univie.ac.at/~gerald/ftp/book-jac/jacop.pdf . Screened official author description/contents: endpoint Weyl m-functions and inverse spectral reconstruction are established tools. Standard reconstruction from a diagonal endpoint Green function does not immediately settle a constrained physical-parameter map from a single adjacent off-diagonal Green function; that conversion is a mathematical step requiring proof. This screening did not perform a theorem-by-theorem comparison of all inverse-Jacobi literature.

## Search boundary and verdict

Queries included catenary+singular locus+adjacent/noncollocated; catenary+identifiability+Jacobi/inverse/continued fraction; tridiagonal+Jacobian+compartmental+identifiability; and the Chen1985 title. Search snippets were used as leads, with the primary statements/abstracts above inspected. No exact displayed factors R3(-p1) or q1R3(-(p1+e))-eT(-(p1+e)) were located in this bounded search.

Verdict: no immediately equivalent theorem found in inspected primary statements. The October2026 rooted-tree claim is explicitly a different port/leak scope. A rational/continued-fraction endpoint inverse is established methodology and must be credited; a proof using it should be framed as a scope extension/application, not a new inverse-spectral method. Historical pharmacokinetic and off-diagonal Green-function literature was not exhausted, so novelty remains provisional.
