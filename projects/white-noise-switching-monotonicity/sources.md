The prose moves between a fixed fast-switching-optimal target and a target optimized at each finite rate. This project freezes the former reading. It proves an explicit finite counterexample and a slow-rate obstruction on that branch, with no assertion about the reoptimized envelope. It preserves the source's broader conclusions that heterogeneity can improve fitness.

The model/fitness convention was checked against the [author implementation](https://github.com/ap-browning/heterogeneity), particularly `library/white-noise/solve_whitenoise.jl`. The final displayed supplementary antiderivative does not transparently match that stationary implementation; the present proofs reconstruct the invariant law from the original population SDE and verify its zero-current identity. ## Equivalent stochastic dispersal model

Evans SN, Ralph PL, Schreiber SJ and Sen A (2013), *Stochastic population growth in spatially heterogeneous environments*, Journal of Mathematical Biology66:423–476, [DOI10.1007/s00285-012-0514-0](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00285-012-0514-0), [author-hosted primary PDF](https://schreiber.faculty.ucdavis.edu/wp-content/uploads/sites/568/2021/04/2013_JMB_StochasticPopulationGrowthInSp-2.pdf). Its abundance SDE Eq3 is exactly the same stochastic system under a change from patch dispersal to phenotype switching. In its row-outflow convention the correspondence is

    D = omega*[[-q,q],[1−q,−(1−q)]],
    Sigma = [[sigma1²,rho*sigma1*sigma2],
             [rho*sigma1*sigma2,sigma2²]].

Thus the abundance drift is diag(mu)*R+D^T*R and the environmental covariance is diag(R)*Sigma*diag(R), reproducing the two population equations in proof.md. Its Theorem1 gives the stationary growth identity, Example1 gives the two-patch invariant density, Proposition2 gives the fast-dispersal limit, Proposition3 gives continuity/analyticity, and Example2 already exhibits generic nonmonotone scaled-dispersal growth. Theorem2 also provides a high-rate expansion, including analysis at ideal allocation. Consequently neither the general model, stationary-law representation, fitness identity nor general rate nonmonotonicity is a novelty claim here.

The finite rational comparison is presented only as a clarification of the specified fixed-fast-optimal-target reading of Browning–Hamis2025. No direct statement of this exact fixed-optimal-target slow-rate expansion or rational witness was located in the inspected prior statements. This bounded observation does not establish priority or a standalone new result; the use proposed here is a neutral author-repository clarification.
