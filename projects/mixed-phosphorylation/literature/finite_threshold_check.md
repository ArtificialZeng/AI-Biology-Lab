# Focused literature comparison for the finite-kinase theorem

Checked on 2026-10-26, Asia/Shanghai. This is a bounded research-stage check;
no claim of priority or comprehensive open status is made.

The proposed delta is the explicit all-K equilibrium-instability certificate
at P=8,S=40: K>=4660166/209569 suffices. It supplies a quantified witness to
Conjecture 6.2(1), without deciding part (2).

Primary sources actually opened in this research pass:

- [Conradi–Mincheva–Shiu, arXiv:1809.02886v2](https://arxiv.org/html/1809.02886v2):
  checked the Table 1 rates, equations (2), conservation laws (3), Proposition
  3.1, the Hurwitz-matrix definition, Figure 5(c), and Conjecture 6.2. The
  paper provides uniqueness and general stability-boundary results and
  numerical large-kinase examples. Its final conjecture explicitly asks for
  instability at arbitrarily large kinase totals. The proposed finite bound
  and certificate address that first assertion. Uniqueness, existence of
  oscillations at some parameters, and the general Hurwitz method are prior
  results, not this project's contribution.
- [Grasse–Conradi, BIOMATH 2025 official abstract](https://biomath.math.bas.bg/biomath/index.php/bmcs/article/download/1787/1594/7828):
  this abstract studies the reverse processive/distributive arrangement. It
  describes prior kinase robustness, citing the 2019 source, but contains no
  finite-kinase threshold or proof for the theorem above. Its broad wording
  does not settle which mathematical notion of robustness has been proved.
- [SBHD 2023 official poster programme](https://www.sbhdberlin.org/pdf/SBHD2023_Poster_Session_Program.pdf):
  poster 48 by Kim-Joao Grasse directly addresses robustness under kinase
  total changes. The programme gives no mathematical theorem or proof.
  This is a direct overlap warning; the poster itself was not obtained.

instability/proof/2026; the original conjecture number with phosphorylation;

These returned the original article, the 2023/2025 records above, and nearby
different models. No equivalent complete proof or explicit bound was located
in the material inspected. A search failure is not proof of absence or
novelty. The earlier broader comparison is retained in `literature/novelty.md`.
