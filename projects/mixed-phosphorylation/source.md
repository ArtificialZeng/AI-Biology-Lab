# Published model and research question

C. Conradi, M. Mincheva, A. Shiu, *Emergence of oscillations in a mixed-mechanism phosphorylation system* (2019), DOI: https://doi.org/10.1007/s11538-019-00580-6. The version used is https://arxiv.org/html/1809.02886v2, equations (1)–(3), Table 1, Figure 5(c), and Conjecture 6.2.

The original conjecture has two assertions. In paraphrase, the first asks for fixed phosphatase and substrate totals whose equilibria remain unstable at arbitrarily large kinase totals. The second concerns persistent periodic orbits at large kinase totals and independence of their S2-coordinate projection from that total. The exact interpretation of the projection statement matters; a period change alone does not negate set-valued coordinate projection invariance.

Figure 5(c) studies total phosphatase 8 and total substrate 40 at total kinase 100, 1000, and 10000. The rate vector is (1,1,1,1,100,1,9/10,3,1,100). Oscillations at selected parameters, equilibrium uniqueness, and the general Hurwitz approach are prior work.

This project's exact result supplies witnesses for the first assertion, with an explicit sufficient bound for every finite kinase total above it. The full conjecture remains unresolved here. Differences observed at one parameter pair cannot silently replace the source's existential quantifiers.
