## Abstract

**Background:** Clinical sequencing laboratories rely on ClinVar classifications across the 84 medically actionable genes in ACMG SF v3.3 to guide preventive surgery, cardiac device implantation, and cascade testing. While gnomAD v4.1 expands reference variation to 807,162 individuals and Johnston et al. (2026, *Genetics in Medicine* 28(5):102552, `PMC13170090`) recently established disease allele frequency thresholds (DAFTs) across secondary-finding genes, automated ClinVar–gnomAD audits remain vulnerable to multiallelic rsID join collisions, unequal ancestry allele-number denominators, chrX non-PAR ploidy mis-filtering, stealth technical QC failures, and unstratified recessive-versus-dominant ClinVar VCF tags.

**Methods:** We joined all 343,328 ClinVar GRCh38 variants across all 84 ACMG SF v3.3 genes to all 545,493 gnomAD v4.1 variants using exact minimal alleles (`CHROM-POS-REF-ALT`; `n = 155,434` matched; independent API `joint.faf95` Pearson `r = 0.9997`). We enforced ploidy- and PAR-aware callability (`AN >= 80%` of `2N = 1,614,324` on autosomes/PAR, and of official gnomAD v4.1 non-PAR chrX `N_XY + 2*N_XX = 400,897 + 2 × 406,265 = 1,213,427` -> `80% = 970,741.6` covering *ABCD1*, *GLA*, and *OTC*) and extracted exome+genome hemizygote counts. We eliminated singleton denominator bias via `FAF95 >= 1e-4` (`n = 10,058`, including `84` chrX variants), `AC >= 10` (`n = 4,932`), and exact equal-AN hypergeometric downsampling (`AN_NFE = AN_p`). To guarantee 100% primary-source threshold traceability, we retrieved all 250 live ClinGen Criteria Specification Registry (CSpec) SVI JSON documents on `2026-11-25` and integrated all **30 officially Released ClinGen CSpec VCEP specifications** (including *APC* `GN089` v2.1 `BS1 = 1e-5`, *PMS2* `GN139` v2.0 `BS1 = 2.8e-4`, *ABCD1* `GN105` v1.0, and *OTC* `GN156` v1.0's official `>= 10` homozygote/hemizygote BA1 criterion) with Johnston et al.'s (2026) **6 preliminary VCEP genes** and **47 published DAFT genes** (excluding single-variant *HFE* and named BA1/BS1 exceptions). We live-audited 100% (`55/55`) of allele-exact 2–4★ AD P/LP alerts and 100% (`18/18`) of flagged X-linked alerts against gnomAD v4.1 exome/genome QC filters, evaluated VUS persistence using collapsible LPM risk differences and non-parametric submitter-tier stratification, and executed a prospectively locked out-of-sample validation across 20 unseen autosomal AD genes (`99,249` ClinVar × `157,021` gnomAD v4.1; `38,023` exact matches).

**Results:**
1. **Multiallelic rsID Join Inflation:** Naive rsID matching inflates `FAF95 >= 1e-4` P/LP alerts by **72.2%** overall (`544` vs `151` exact) and **79.7%** among 2–4★ AD alerts (`271` vs `55` exact), replicating prospectively at **84.6% / 80.2%** in the 20 unseen AD genes (`149` vs `23` / `96` vs `19`), converging with Lai et al.'s (2026) 75.0% epilepsy FPR.
2. **Retraction of Spurious Aggregate P/LP Disparity & Robust Non-NFE VUS Persistence:** Raw `AF >= 1e-4` screening pulls 64.7% `AC = 1` singletons in small-AN `MID` (`median AN = 6,006` vs NFE `1,179,788`), inducing an artificial P/LP disparity that collapses completely under ploidy- and denominator-clean `FAF95 >= 1e-4` (`n = 10,058`: `0.82% vs 0.67%`, CMH `OR = 1.55, p = 0.391`; LPM `+0.28 pp, p = 0.437`), equal-AN hypergeometric downsampling (`OR = 0.42–1.27`), and unseen 20-gene validation (`OR = 0.36, p = 0.169`). Conversely, non-NFE VUS persistence survives every clean test (ACMG 84 ploidy-aware: `12.01% vs 6.86%`, CMH `OR = 2.62, p = 2.03e-9`; collapsible LPM `+8.86 pp (SE 1.04), p = 1.96e-17`; hypergeometric ORs `1.62–2.72`, all `p <= 1.3e-5`; unseen 20-gene LPM `+10.84 pp, p = 5.93e-6`), while Conflicting classifications are lower in non-NFE (`43.16% vs 63.38%`, CMH `OR = 0.47, p = 1.56e-18`). Within every fixed submitter-count tier (`1, 2–3, 4–9, 10–19, 20+`), non-NFE and NFE VUS rates are indistinguishable (all Fisher `p >= 0.16`), reflecting concentration in sparse `1–3` submitter tiers; conditioning on submitter-tier bins descriptively attenuates the LPM gap by **55.5%** (`+8.86 -> +3.94 pp, p = 3.24e-4`; continuous `log(1+subs)`: `-0.14 pp, p = 0.899`; unseen 20-gene: **50.0%**), strictly framed as descriptive covariate attenuation rather than causal mediation.
3. **Complete 55/55 AD & chrX Live-QC + Official CSpec/DAFT + MOI Triage:** **0/55 (0.0%)** AD 2–4★ alerts exceed verified BA1 and **0/55** represent unambiguous frequency-only AD P/LP misclassifications: `8/55` (`14.5%`) are gnomAD QC/callability artifacts (7 low-AN + stealth `AS_VQSR` failure in *APC* `5-112775626-T-A` at `93.9%` AN), `22/55` (`40.0%`) FIN/ASJ bottleneck founders, `14/55` (`25.5%`) established continental founders/reduced-penetrance/pseudogene, `6/55` (`10.9%`) genuine AD pathogenic alleles strictly below verified BS1, `1/55` (`1.8%`) reduced-penetrance pharmacogenetic *CACNA1S* p.Arg1086Ser above Johnston DAFT BS1 (`5e-5`) but below BA1 (`5e-4`), and **`4/55` (`7.3%`) AR-in-dual-mode-AD return hazards** (*RYR1* p.Arg3550Trp, *RYR1* c.10348-6C>G [explicitly listed as an AR congenital myopathy BA1/BS1 exclusion in ClinGen CSpec `GN179` v2.0], *RYR1* p.Ile2781fs, and *TTN* p.Lys25729Ter). On non-PAR chrX, the sole 2★ P/LP alert (*GLA* `X-101398011-C-T`, p.Arg363His, `AMR = 0.000219`, `18` hemizygotes) passes live QC and lies strictly below Johnston DAFT BS1 (`0.000363`).
4. **Ploidy-Aware chrX Recovery & Verified CSpec/DAFT VUS Queue (`n = 51,190` VUS across 83 genes):** Fixing autosomal-vs-chrX callability recovers **`406/441` X-linked VUS** (*ABCD1* `200/207`, *GLA* `117/137`, *OTC* `89/97`; `0` rows differ vs earlier `1,214,237` estimate across all `1,615` chrX rows) and recovers **`17` flagged X-linked VUS**: `11` *ABCD1* (`GN105`, including `X-153729352-G-T` > BA1 with `17` hemizygotes, `X-153743749-G-C` failing `AS_VQSR`+`segdup`, and `4` more flagged `segdup`), **`5` *OTC* VUS meeting `GN156` v1.0's official `>= 10` male-hemizygote BA1 criterion (`12–55` hemizygotes, 5/5 live-QC PASS)**, and `1` *GLA* VUS (`X-101397903-C-G`, `19` hemizygotes, PASS). Strictly within the **30 officially Released ClinGen CSpec VCEP genes**, **327 high-callability VUS exceed official BS1/BA1** (`77.7%` NFE-invisible: `82` SAS, `75` NFE, `67` EAS, `61` AFR, `42` AMR; `278` with `AC >= 5`; `322` frequency-only; `247` uniform `FAF95`; `320` excluding *BRCA1/2*) and **21 exceed BA1** (`90.5%` NFE-invisible: `16` frequency-only + `5` *OTC* hemizygotes), led by *APC* `GN089` (`107`), *FBN1* `GN022` (`93`), *HNF1A* `GN017` (`22`), *VHL* `GN078` (`13`), *ABCD1* `GN105` (`11`), *PTEN* `GN003` (`10`), and *OTC* `GN156` (`5`). Adding 6 preliminary VCEP genes (`99` BS1 / `9` BA1) and 47 Johnston DAFT genes (`962` BS1 / `216` BA1) yields **`1,388` BS1/BA1** (`55.1%` NFE-invisible; `1,224` `AC >= 5`) and **`246` BA1** (`77.2%` NFE-invisible) across all 83 thresholded genes (combined live gnomAD QC audit across `20` autosomal + all `17` chrX VUS: **`36/37` = 97.3% PASS**). Exceeding BS1/BA1 prioritizes candidates for formal VCEP review rather than automatic benign reclassification.

## Limitations & Explicit Scope Boundaries

1. *BRCA1* (`GN092`) and *BRCA2* (`GN097`) VCEP specifications reference gnomAD non-cancer subsets; excluding both leaves Tier 1 virtually identical (`320` vs `327` BS1/BA1; `21` vs `21` BA1).
2. Tier 2 (6 preliminary VCEPs) and Tier 3 (47 Johnston DAFT genes) provide interim guidance pending formal ClinGen VCEP release, and ultra-low DAFTs (*MYH11*, *WT1*) can trigger BS1 at `AC < 5`; we report tier-stratified counts and `AC >= 5` sensitivities transparently.
3. Live gnomAD QC filter gating was performed exhaustively on 55/55 AD P/LP alerts and all 18/18 flagged chrX variants (catching 1 *ABCD1* `AS_VQSR`+`segdup` failure and 4 *ABCD1* `segdup` paralog flags), and via stratified 20-variant spot-checking (`20/20` PASS) on autosomal VUS candidates (`36/37 = 97.3%` combined VUS PASS); clinical pipelines must gate every variant on full filter and `segdup` flags prior to curation.
4. Cross-sectional ClinVar submitter-count adjustments are strictly descriptive covariate decompositions and do not identify causal mediation (Imai et al. 2010; Mood 2010).
5. This study evaluates population allele frequencies and public registry metadata rather than patient-level penetrance or functional assays.
