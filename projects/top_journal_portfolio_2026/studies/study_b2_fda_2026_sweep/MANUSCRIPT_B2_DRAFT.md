## Abstract

**Motivation:** Regulatory transparency regimes increasingly rely on low-cost administrative outreach rather than formal prosecutions, yet empirical evidence on how organizational governance mediates compliance—and whether overdue trials conceal systematically worse clinical outcomes—remains limited. On **March 30, 2026** (publicly documented in an official FDA GovDelivery bulletin released **April 13, 2026**: `https://content.govdelivery.com/accounts/USFDA/bulletins/412bbc8`), the FDA issued mass delinquency reminders to over 2,200 sponsors covering more than 3,000 overdue Applicable Clinical Trials (ACTs) under FDAAA Section 801 (42 CFR Part 11).

**Methods:** Using the complete ClinicalTrials.gov REST API v2 snapshot pulled `2026-11-25` (`53,736` trials; `16,333` strict-`ACTUAL` `COMPLETED|TERMINATED` ACTs), we implement five forensic methodological corrections:
1. **T00 (NLM 2020-01-29 QC Policy & Unposted Tracking Proxy):** Distinguishing QC-cleared `resultsFirstSubmitDate` (`13,154` strict ACTs) from unposted in-QC `estimatedResultsFirstSubmitDate` (`817` strict ACTs; verified `100.0%` identical [`0.0d` diff] to `earliest_subinfo_release_date` across all `817/817` strict ACTs and `2,026/2,026` all arms, with `0` fallback records and `0` mixing of `331` delayed-certification-only records). Because late-November submissions not yet entered into tracking cannot be observed, this measure is labeled the **Combined Posted + Unposted Tracking Proxy** and paired with date-verified zero-censoring composite outcomes.
2. **T01 (Strict Calendar-Date Zero-Censoring Uniform Horizons):** Composite outcome ("submitted *and* cleared QC within `<= H` days") strictly enforcing `end_date + H <= 2026-11-25` (`H=90d` thru Jul 31 [`27d` margin] / exact Aug 27; `H=120d` thru Jun 30 [`28d` margin] / exact Jul 28; `H=150d` thru May 31 [`28d` margin] / exact Jun 28).
3. **T07, T06 & T10 (Fixed Pre-Window Invariance, Official CRAN R `HonestDiD` v0.2.8 & Grid-Boundary Expansion):** Because raw Within-ACT Industry-vs-Academic overdue DiD fails monthly pre-trends (`Wald df=10, stat=32.225, p=0.000367`), primary identification rests on **Overdue-vs.-On-Time** within-sector DiD and DDD designs with calendar-locked pre-window `2025-04..2026-02` (`df=10`, invariant across `ref=2026-02 / 2025-12 / 2026-03`, `max diff <= 4.9e-12`). All 15 contiguous specs are evaluated via official CRAN R **`HonestDiD` v0.2.8** (`Delta^SD FLCI` and `Delta^RM C-LF`). Direct boundary auditing revealed 5 outer-$\bar{M}$ C-LF rows hitting the default $\pm 20\,\mathrm{SE}$ grid edge (`quarterly_within_act Q2–Q3 Mbar=2` and 4 monthly 6m averages at `Mbar=1`, all already containing zero); all 5 were recomputed on expanded $\pm 40\,\mathrm{SE}$ grids (`1,001` points, both boundaries rejected, `0/15` remaining boundary hits) with `1,001`-vs.-`2,001` convergence verification. All C-LF endpoints are grid-resolution approximations (monthly default step `0.11–0.15` log-IRR; expanded step `0.015–0.029`), not exact real-valued bounds.
4. **T03/T04 (Post-Hoc Exploratory Power & Margin Sensitivity on H4 Outcomes):** Within-sponsor FE regressions across `5,064` FDA-regulated disclosures (`n = 4,224` across `555` sponsors; primary p-value subsample `n = 1,035` across `184` sponsors) paired with 80%-power MDEs, **post-hoc exploratory** `±5 pp` / `±10 pp` TOST margin sensitivities (chosen after viewing pilot/initial CIs rather than pre-specified clinical equivalence thresholds), and QC selection checks.

**Results:**
- **Primary Governance Asymmetry:** Industry Overdue-vs.-On-Time DiD passes pre-trends cleanly (monthly `Wald df=10, p=0.4293`; quarterly `df=3, p=0.3044`) and increases across every post window (`Apr–Jun IRR = 1.574, p = 0.00054`; `Apr–Jul 1.469, p = 0.00173`; `Apr–Sep 1.361, p = 0.00416`; `Apr–Oct 1.399, p = 0.00115`). Academic Overdue-vs.-On-Time DiD shows no statistically detectable lift in any window (`0.858–0.998`, all `p >= 0.28`). Overdue-vs.-On-Time DDD passes pre-trends at 5% (`df=10, p=0.0589`; quarterly `df=3, p=0.1203`) and is significant across every post window (`Apr–Jun 1.585, p_spon=0.0234, p_2way=0.00345`; `Apr–Jul 1.589, p_spon=0.0165, p_2way=0.00296`; `Apr–Sep 1.598 [1.128, 2.264], p_spon=0.00831, p_2way=0.00251`, 1,000-perm sponsor RI `p = 0.010`; `Apr–Oct 1.521, p_spon=0.0145, p_2way=0.00605`; excluding `2,219` delayed-certification trials: `IRR = 2.288 [1.547, 3.383], p = 3.37e-5`; Clean-Exempt DDD `2.486, p = 0.00094`; 2025/2024 placebos indistinguishable from unity at `0.964 / 0.860`, `p >= 0.41`). Within-ACT Overdue DiD is imprecise in short windows (`Apr–Jun 1.262, p=0.134`; `Apr–Jul 1.315, p=0.0635`) after recovering `13` unposted April Academic in-QC spikes, reaching significance only at 6–7m (`Apr–Sep 1.429, p_spon=0.0095`).
- **T01 Date-Verified Zero-Censoring Composite Horizons:** Significant Industry OD/OT lifts across all 6 windows (`1.46–1.69`, all `p <= 0.0066`) and 5/6 DDDs (`H=90d` full-month `1.960, p=0.0157`; `H=120d` `1.654, p=0.0436`; exact-day `1.999, 1.783, 1.722`, all `p <= 0.0324`); full-month `H=150d` DDD (`1.523, p_spon=0.153`) and `H=120d/150d` Within-ACT (`1.278/1.125, p >= 0.21`) are non-significant; 2025/2024 `H=120d` placebos are indistinguishable from unity (`1.169/0.906`).
- **T06/T10 Official CRAN R `HonestDiD` v0.2.8 Partial-Identification Limits:** Quarterly `2026Q2` OT/OD DDD (`2.023`) and Industry OD/OT (`1.620`) exclude zero through `Mbar = 0.25` (`Delta^RM C-LF`) and `M = 0.10 / 0.05` (`Delta^SD FLCI`), **but cross zero at `Mbar = 0.50` (approximate `lb = -0.016` and `-0.028` log-IRR)**. Quarterly `Q2–Q3` Industry OD/OT average (`1.385`) **already includes zero at `Mbar = 0` (`[-0.010, +0.663]`)**, and March-referenced monthly averages include zero (`1.075` and `1.712`). Expanded $\pm 40\,\mathrm{SE}$ (`1,001`-pt) recomputation of all 5 boundary-hitting outer-$\bar{M}$ rows confirms all 5 remain wide intervals containing zero with both search boundaries rejected.
- **Mechanisms & Phase-1 Spillover:** Lift concentrates in small commercial sponsors (`1–2` FDA trials: `1.841, p = 0.00026`; large `20+` already at `8.22%/mo`, `1.090 [0.745, 1.595], p = 0.657`), recently overdue `2023–25` cohorts (`1.500, p = 0.00074`; no statistically detectable lift in legacy `2017–20` `0.913 [0.362, 2.301], p = 0.847`), and all industry backlog tiers (`2.961, 2.826, 2.029`, all `p <= 0.0094`). Delayed-results certification filings showed `0` observed post-sweep events (`4` total), providing no evidence of formal certification substitution without proving the absence of other unmeasured deferral practices. **Within-firm spillover to statutorily exempt Phase 1 trials is not statistically detectable (`IRR = 0.893 [0.407, 1.957], p = 0.777`, `n = 3,910` trials, `125` events)** — explicitly retracting the pre-audit raw-April spillover claim while noting the wide CI cannot rule out modest spillovers.
- **T03/T04 H4 Clinical Outcome Audit:** Within sponsors, observed post-sweep overdue disclosures lie inside **post-hoc exploratory `±5 pp` TOST margins** for **mortality (`+0.71 pp [-2.11, +3.53], MDE80 = 4.03 pp, p = 0.0014`)** and **SAE rates (`+0.39 pp [-2.68, +3.45], MDE80 = 4.38 pp, p = 0.0016`)**, with no statistically detectable excess termination in unposted in-QC trials (`20.33% vs 21.86%, Fisher p = 0.736`). However, **primary-outcome non-significance (`p >= 0.05`: `-0.14 pp [-16.13, +15.85], MDE80 = 22.86 pp`) is severely underpowered**, TOST margins are post-hoc exploratory rather than pre-specified, and observed disclosures do not exclude strategic concealment among still-undisclosed trials.

## Explicit Scientific Boundaries

1. **Partial-Identification & Grid-Resolution Limits (T06/T10):** Official CRAN R `HonestDiD` v0.2.8 bounds cross zero at $\bar{M}=0.50$ (quarterly Q2) and at $\bar{M}=0$ for quarterly Q2–Q3 Industry OD/OT average and March-referenced monthly averages; C-LF bounds are discrete-grid approximations (`0/15` boundary hits after expanding all 5 affected outer-$\bar{M}$ entries to $\pm 40\,\mathrm{SE}$, `1,001` pts).
2. **Within-ACT Pre-Trend Failure:** Raw Within-ACT Industry-vs.-Academic overdue DiD fails monthly pre-trends (`p = 0.000367`) and is imprecise in short 3–4m windows once unposted April academic records are recovered; primary inference relies on Overdue-vs.-On-Time designs.
3. **Proxy-Date & Non-Detection Boundaries:** `estimatedResultsFirstSubmitDate` matched `earliest_subinfo_release_date` on `100.0%` of `2,026` records (`0` fallback, `0` cert mixing), but very recent November submissions not yet entered into tracking cannot be observed; zero observed post-sweep delayed certifications does not prove the absence of other unmeasured deferral practices; and non-significant CIs on Phase-1 (`[0.41, 1.96]`), legacy cohorts (`[0.36, 2.30]`), and historical placebos represent absence of statistically detectable effects rather than exact zeros.
4. **Intent-to-Treat Scope:** FDA did not publish recipient identities (`fda2026bulletin`); estimates represent ITT effects across statutorily overdue ACTs.
5. **Post-Hoc TOST & Undisclosed-Trial Limits:** The `±5 pp` / `±10 pp` TOST margins are post-hoc exploratory sensitivities rather than pre-specified clinical equivalence tests; primary efficacy non-significance (`MDE80 = 22.86 pp`) is severely underpowered; and observed disclosures cannot exclude strategic concealment among trials that remained undisclosed.
