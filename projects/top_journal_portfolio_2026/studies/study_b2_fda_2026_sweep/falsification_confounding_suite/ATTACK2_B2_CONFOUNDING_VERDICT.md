## Executive Verdict (Sharply Bounded — Diffuse Acute `Mar 30–Apr 12` & Q1-Donut `Apr 1–12` Private-Email Jump Survives Period FE and Posted-Only Data, While Within-Sponsor-Period FE, Cliff-Free Controls, and Post-Apr-13 Posted-Only Ind OD/OT Show Material Fragility)

1. **TEST 2A — EXACT DAILY & WEEKLY TIMING (`2026-03-30` Private Emails vs. `2026-04-13` Public Press Release):**
   - **Pre-Sweep March (`Mar 1–29, 2026`):** Flat (`Ind OD/OT IRR = 1.0350 [0.6250, 1.7140], p = 0.8936`; `OT/OD DDD = 0.7740, p = 0.4643`).
   - **Same-Day `2026-03-30..31` Jump:** `9/39` (`23.1%`) of March Industry Overdue (`13.29%/30d`) and `8/44` (`18.2%`) of March Academic Overdue (`21.90%/30d`) occurred in those final 2 days, while Industry On-Time remained flat (`5.32% -> 5.53%/30d`).
   - **Private-Email-Only Window (`2026-03-30..2026-04-12`, strictly before the April 13 public press release):** Under Period FE, **`Ind OD/OT IRR = 2.7120 [1.3975, 5.2629], p = 0.00318`**, diffused across **`29` distinct industry sponsors (`31` trials, max `2` trials per sponsor)**. Academic Overdue also jumped immediately (`Sponsor × Period FE = 2.6793, p = 0.0482`), explaining why `OT/OD DDD = 1.5135 (p = 0.3549)` during those first two weeks.

2. **TEST 2D & 2E — Q1 QUARTER-END PLACEBO (`2024`/`2025` vs. `2026`), Q1-DONUT (`Apr 1–12`), AND POSTED-ONLY SENSITIVITY:**
   - **No Generic Calendar Quarter-End Artifact:** Historical `Mar 30–31` showed zero/minimal Industry Overdue (`2024`: `0` events [Sat–Sun]; `2025`: `1` event, `1.54%/30d`), and Q2/Q3/Q4 quarter-ends across `2024–2025` showed no systematic overdue bunching (`Ind OD` QE-to-month-baseline ratio median `= 0.68×`, range `0.30×–1.61×`). Historical `2025 Apr 1–12` placebo was completely flat (`Ind OD/OT IRR = 0.5622, p = 0.123`; `Acad OD/OT = 0.9398, p = 0.819`).
   - **Honest Caution From `2024 Apr 1–12`:** In `2024 Apr 1–12`, Industry Overdue spiked to `40` events (`10.79%/30d`, raw `Ind OD/OT = 3.46`), **driven heavily by a single-sponsor bulk upload (`ImmunityBio, Inc.`, `10` trials in 3 days ahead of its April 22, 2024 FDA BLA approval; `24` sponsors total)**—contrasting sharply with `2026 Mar 30–Apr 12`'s `29`-sponsor diffusion, yet demonstrating how short-window rates can be perturbed by idiosyncratic sponsor events.
   - **Q1-Donut Pure Private-Email Window (`2026-04-01..2026-04-12` — excludes BOTH `Mar 30–31` Q1-end AND `Apr 13+` public press release):**
     - **Period FE (Combined Proxy):** Survives for BOTH **`Ind OD/OT = 2.3605 [1.1716, 4.7559], p = 0.0163`** and **`Acad OD/OT = 2.1213 [1.0393, 4.3300], p = 0.0388`** (`OT/OD DDD = 1.1166, p = 0.829` because both groups surged!).
     - **Period FE (Posted-Only `results_first_submit_date`, zero unposted-tracking imputation):** **`Ind OD/OT = 2.6575 [1.2840, 5.5002], p = 0.00845`** (`Acad OD/OT = 1.8608, p = 0.103`), confirming the acute `Apr 1–12` Industry surge is **not** driven by unposted-tracking date imputation.
     - **Honest Fragilities Preserved Verbatim:**
       - Under **`Sponsor × Period FE`** in `Apr 1–12`, **`Ind OD/OT` collapses to `0.8010 [0.1633, 3.9297], p = 0.784`** (`0.9883, p = 0.988` posted-only), whereas `Acad OD/OT` holds `4.5145 [1.0069, 20.2407], p = 0.0490` (`3.1224, p = 0.102` posted-only); `Mar 30–31` suffers complete separation under Industry `Sponsor × Period FE` (`0` On-Time events in dual-status 2-day firm cells).
       - In **`PUBLIC_PRESS` (`Apr 13–Sep 30`) on posted-only data**, `OT/OD DDD = 1.9254 [1.2506, 2.9644], p = 0.0029` is driven primarily by `Acad OD/OT` dropping (`0.6407, p = 0.0118`), whereas **`Ind OD/OT` attenuates to non-significant `1.2131 [0.9383, 1.5683], p = 0.140`** (vs. `1.2786, p = 0.0339` on combined proxy).

3. **TEST 2B & 2C — 6-MONTH HIGH-DIMENSIONAL FE, ON-TIME CLIFF, DONUT-MARCH, FINE AGE, AND LOSO:**
   - **Survives:** `Therapeutic-Area × Month FE` (`DDD = 1.5900, p = 0.0084`), `Phase × Intv × Month FE` (`DDD = 1.6364, p = 0.0048`), Donut-March (`DDD = 1.5895, p = 0.0113`), Fine 1-month age FE (`DDD = 1.5838, p = 0.0098`), Top-20 LOSO (`DDD ∈ [1.5161, 1.6463], max p = 0.0165`).
   - **Attenuates / Fails Significance (Preserved Verbatim):**
     - **`Sponsor × Month FE`** (`908` dual-status firm-months): **`DDD = 1.0975 [0.6244, 1.9292], p = 0.7464`**; `Ind OD/OT = 1.1991, p = 0.3657` (driven largely by dual-status multi-trial firms showing `1.1609, p = 0.336` even with plain Month FE vs. single-status firms `1.6359, p = 0.00143`). Strictly predetermined intra-firm spillover (`>=1` overdue ACT on `2025-03-31`) is non-significant (`1.2533, p = 0.0739`; avoiding the mechanical `2026-03-30` survival-conditioning artifact of `2.265`).
     - **Cliff-Free On-Time Controls (`0–9m` / `0–6m`, dropping `9–12m` deadline rush):** **`0–9m` `DDD = 1.3179 [0.7446, 2.3325], p = 0.3433`** (`Ind OD/OT = 1.3569, p = 0.1286`); `0–6m` `DDD = 1.2499, p = 0.5894`.
